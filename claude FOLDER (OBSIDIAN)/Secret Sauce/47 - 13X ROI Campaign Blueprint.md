## 2 Major Things to Make This Happen

To make this work, you need to understand the concept of retargeting and making a compelling funnel.

Let's start!

## 1) Retargeting

We have this we call the Traffic Temperature

- **Cold** – people who don't know you at all and mostly don't care a thing about your offer until they will be targeted by your ad.
- **Warm** – Has engaged in one of your ads, sees your page, and is familiar with what you're offering and doing.
- **Hot** – Has bought one of your products, liked your page, follows you and is active in liking and commenting on your page posts.

**Remember:**

**FITFU** = Fortune Is in The Follow Up

Because 80% of the buyers buy on the 5th to 12th exposure of your product or service.

Your goal is to show and show the ad until they hit the buy button.

## 2) Converting Sales Funnel

This works on both high and low ticket products but it works well only if you've instructed it better.

There are 3 pages in a regular webinar funnel but we'll go through all the advanced stuff and discuss **6 webinar funnel steps.**

1. **Webinar Registration**
   - This is an option page where they will be typing their name and email address to register for the webinar

2. **Thank You Page**
   - A page wherein you will acknowledge them for taking actions. It's either a video or a photo and also instructions on what to do next.
   - Letting them feel excited so that they will come to the webinar.

3. **Webinar Room**
   - This is where the webinar happens
   - There are 2 types of webinar:
     - Live Webinar – where you'll be doing it live.
     - Recorded Webinar – recorded video that seems to be live.

4. **Webinar Replay**
   - Where you will be putting the recorded video of the live webinar so that those who failed to attend the webinar may have the chance to watch it again. This is where you can scope up profits without doing the webinar again.

5. **Checkout Page**
   - Where they will type their credit card details to buy your product.
   - The payment methods are all on this page.

6. **Confirmation Page**
   - Where you will confirm their payment
   - The same with the thank you page.

## Retargeting

We have this something we call the Traffic Temperature

- **Visit the Webinar Registration but did not continue to the Thank You Page,** in short they did not sign up.

  Now, that person becomes a warm audience and that's where we can create an ad targeting them.

- **Visits Thank You Page but did not visit the Webinar Room/Webinar Replay**

  Now, that person becomes a warm audience and that's where we can create an ad targeting them.

- **Visited the Webinar/Replay but did not visit the Checkout Page.**

  You can run a retargeting ad targeting these people and crushing their objection and self limiting beliefs as to why they did not proceed to the next page.

- **Visits Checkout Page but did not visit Confirmation Page**

  They visited the checkout but did not continue to purchase the product. So you run a retargeting ad, specifically a testimonial ad.

- **Visits Confirmation Page**

  Since they are already a hot audience, you can retarget them and sell them another product and retarget them again and again if you have another product to sell or launch.

To make this happen, you need a **Facebook Pixel**.

**Facebook Pixel** – is a snippet of code that Facebook will give you and all you need to do is copy and paste it in your funnel. To make it easier, let's call it a basic code.

You're also gonna put an event code.

**Event Code** – a specific code wherein you can tell if that person is a lead, or if the person already bought a product, he is already a buyer.

*Example: You're gonna put the code in your thank you page - so that triggers facebook that if somebody goes into your thank you page, he will be considered as a lead.*

Facebook will then optimize your ad after you put these code because you're now clear with your goal.

## Email Marketing

Since you already captured their personal information including name and email it's time to make use of it through follow-up.

The funnel has been set up and we must follow-up those people who register for the webinar as well as those people who bought your product.

This makes the follow-up a lot easier. You can set it all up so that it will automatically be sent to them.

In the email, you'll be thanking them and giving them the link at the end of the email.

The link should redirect to the Thank You Page and automatically it will redirect them to the webinar room.

Then you can set up another one, after one day an email will be sent automatically thanking them for attending the webinar and sending the webinar replay page link.

And that's how you set-up an email automation.

## Indoctrination Sequence

These are sets of videos that you want them to watch while waiting for the webinar.

The purpose of the videos is to break their self-limiting beliefs and you're building a relationship ahead of the webinar. When the webinar comes they will now have a higher chance to buy your product.
