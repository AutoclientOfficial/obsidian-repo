# Asking For Feedback

> Part of the [[00 - Secret Sauce Index|Secret Sauce]] course. See also [[Sales MOC]] · [[Marketing MOC]].

> Hello Good Morning/Afternoon. May I speak po with *(Customer's Name)*
>
> This is *(Agent)* from Recell! I hope this is a good time to call you po?
>
> Would like to ask feedback lang po sa pag gamit natin kay Recell, 2 mins of your time lang po maam, okay lang po ba?
>
> Kamusta po ang paggamit natin kay Recell?

## IF GOOD FEEDBACK

> Wow! That's good to hear po, Yan din po yung mga kadalasang feedback na naririnig ko po maam *(smiley)*

**1. Ask the client to reorder:**

**2. Ask for testimonial video:** proceed sa testimonial spiel

> Ok lang po ba makahingi ng video testimonial para makatulong sa nangangailangan po nito?
>
> As a gift, mabibigyan po namin kayo ng 1 month supply ng Recell worth P2,000 po!
>
> *Note: give them another 1 bottle if makapag send within 24 hours*
>
> Once na makapag send po kayo ng video, and i approve po sya ng management. Tatawagan ko po kayo agad para mainform kayo na na-qualified yung pinasa nyong video and ipa process na po namin ang ipapadala namin na gift sa inyo. Okay po ba yun maam/sir ______?
>
> Eto po ang mga guidelines natin para sa video testimony. Eto po ang mga questions na kailangan nyo pong sagutin sa video.
>
> You can start by:
>
> 1. Introducing yourself (Name, Age, City)
> 2. Tell us what are your pains before taking ReCell.
> 3. How does ReCell change your life?
> 4. Anong kaibahan ni Recell sa ibang supplement sa market?
> 5. Would you recommend ReCell?
>
> Para po mabilis sya maaprove maam/sir advise ko lang po
>
> 1. Kailangan po No background noise.
> 2. Dapat po maliwanag yung paligid para po makita namin kayo sa video
>
> And pwede nyo po syang isend sa viber ko +63_________ or sa email ko ____@recellofficial.com or pwede din po sa messenger namin sa Fb page.
>
> And para po hindi nyo makalimutan. Magsesend po ako sa inyo ng text after this call ng Guidelines natin for testimony para po may guide kayo. Okay maam/sir?

**3. For inactive clients:**

> Offer reorder: "buy 1 bottle and get 2nd bottle at 50% off" - ito yung ma sesave natin P1,000. Yung babayaran po natin maam instead of P3,990 ito nalang po P2,993

*(minsan wala pa budget ang customer, so iisipin pa nila kung kailan sila makakapag order ulit. Pilitin mo na iinform sila sa TESTIMONY Gifts)*

## IF BAD FEEDBACK

> **Maam if saakin din po yan mangyari, i will also feel the same way.** Im sorry to hear that po ma'am/sir, ino note ko po ito makakarating po ito sa management. makakaasa po kayo na masosolusyunan po itong problem na ito.
>
> Is there anything else na pwede ko pong maitulong?
>
> If wala na
>
> END CALL
>
> *"Thank you po for taking my call, Have a nice day!"*
>
> OR
>
> *"Pasensya na din po sa abala, maraming salamat po. Have a Good day!"*

## IF YOU'RE ASKING FOR LACK OF DETAILS

> Hello Good Morning/Afternoon. May I speak po with *(Customer's Name)*
>
> This is *(Agent)* from Recell! I hope this is a good time to call you po?
>
> Regarding lang po sana sa order ninyong ReCell. Ask ko lang po sana yung complete address nyo? *(kung address lang yung kulang)*
>
> Or confirm ko lang po kung ilang bottles po ang order nyo? *(kung bottle order ang kulang)*

*If magalit ang customer kase tinatanong mo*

> Hindi po kase complete yung information na nareceive namin ma'am/sir?
> Or baka po nagka glitch yung system. Pasensya na po.

*If nabigay na ng customer yung mga kailangan mong information*

> Okay po. This is noted. Confirm ko po full addrress nyo is ________ and email nyo po ay ___________ and full name po ninyo ay ____________ and ang order nyo po ay _____ bottle.
>
> Process ko po after this call Maam/Sir yung order nyo. Thank you po for providing me your information and for your cooperation.
>
> May questions po ba kayo saken?
>
> IF WALA NA
>
> Thank you for your time and for taking my call. Have a nice Day!
