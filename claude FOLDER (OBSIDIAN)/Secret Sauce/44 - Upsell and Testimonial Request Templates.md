## 1. SMS and Messenger Upsell Template

**Subject: Limited Time Offer - Save More with Our 3+1 Promo!**

**Message:**

> Thank you, [First Name]! :)
>
> By the way, baka gusto nyo po e avail ang aming 3+1 promo para mas maka-SAVE po tayo?
>
> If mag-order po kayo ng 3 bottles, meron kayong 1 bottle for FREE! Makaka-save po kayo ng additional P2,000! Pero limited time offer lang po ito...
>
> Gusto nyo po ba mag-order ng 3 bottles, [Name]?

## 2. Asking for Testimonials

## Instructions for Use:

- **Personalization:** Replace [First Name] and [Name] with the recipient's actual name.
- **Timing:** Use the upsell template immediately after a customer makes a purchase or inquiry.
- **Follow-Up:** Send the testimonial request after the customer has used the product and is satisfied with the results. *(normally after 30 days)*

## Notes:

- Ensure the offer details and pricing are up-to-date before sending.
- Customize the messages based on your audience's preferences and feedback.
- Keep a friendly and conversational tone to maintain engagement.
