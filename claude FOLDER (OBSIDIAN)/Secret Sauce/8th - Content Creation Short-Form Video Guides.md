## TEMPLATE 1: Basic Script

A straightforward template to get started with clear, concise messaging that captures attention and delivers your key points effectively.

**Template:**

>
> **[Share a quick story that they can relate too]**
>
> Alam ko nakakarelate ka.
>
> In that case, I have the perfect thing for you...
>
> **[PRODUCT]** is a **[DESCRIPTION]** that helps you to **[SOLVE PROBLEM]**
>
> It is super easy to use..
>
> **[DEMO/Share How to Use]**
>
> It is effective because of **[Unique Selling Proposition]**
>
> So kung gusto mong **[DESIRE]** then eto na yung solution.
>

## TEMPLATE 2: UGC Script

Tailored for user-generated content, this template helps you leverage authentic, relatable testimonials and experiences to build trust and engagement.

**Template:**

> Hi! Ako nga pala si **[NAME]**
>
> Dati **[Share PAINS and PROBLEMS]**
>
> Dahil dito **[Share Consequence of NOT Solving Problem]**
>
> Until nadiskubre ko si **[PRODUCT]**
>
> Yung nagustuhan ko sa **[PRODUCT]** ay dahil **[USP]**
>
> Ngayon **[Share DESIRED RESULTS]**
>
> Kaya kung ikaw may **[PROBLEM]**, try mo rin si **[PRODUCT]**.
>
> Click mo yung yellow basket to order now.
