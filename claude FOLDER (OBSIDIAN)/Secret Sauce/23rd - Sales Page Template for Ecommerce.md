## Headline

- Write 3-5 Headline Variation here
- Guidelines:
  - Who is your target market?
  - What is the Main Problem your product will solve?
  - What is the Main Desire your product will help achieve?
  - What is the Unique Mechanism or USP of your product?
  - What is the level of awareness of your Target Market?
  - What is the level of market sophistication of your product?
- **Headline formula you can use:**
  - How to Achieve **[Desire]** without **[Problem]** using **[USP]**
  - How to Achieve **[Desire]** without **[Problem]** even if you are **[Excuses]**
- Or use AI to help generate Headline Ideas for you

## Solution/Benefits

- Write at least 6 features and benefits of the product
- Use the formula:
  - Feature: **[Insert Feature here]**
  - Benefits: This helps **[what it does]** so that **[what it means or transformation of the target market]**
- Or use AI to help you generate a Features and Benefits list.

Example — Health Benefits of Dr. Green's Veggie Chips:

- **Rich in Nutrients** — The chips contain a mix of carrots, pumpkin, sweet potato, purple sweet potato, and green beans, providing a range of essential nutrients like vitamins, minerals, and antioxidants.
- **Lower Oil Content** — With 70% less oil compared to traditional frying methods, Dr. Green's Veggie Chips offer a healthier snacking option by reducing the intake of saturated fats and bad cholesterol.
- **Vegetable Fiber** — A good source of dietary fiber, which is beneficial for digestive health, weight management, and blood sugar control.
- **No Unhealthy Additives** — As a snack made from real vegetables and no preservatives, the chips contain no unhealthy additives commonly found in traditional processed snacks.
- **Antioxidant Benefits** — The diverse vegetable blend may contribute to antioxidant intake, which can help combat oxidative stress and support overall health.
- **Make Healthy Eating Easy** — Dr. Green's Veggie Chips offer a hassle-free way to add vegetables to your diet, especially for those who struggle to eat enough during regular meals.
