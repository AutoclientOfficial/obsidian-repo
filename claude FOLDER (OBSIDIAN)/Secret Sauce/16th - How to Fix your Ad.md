# How To Fix Your Ad

> Part of the [[00 - Secret Sauce Index|Secret Sauce]] course. See also [[Sales MOC]] · [[Marketing MOC]].

## Who Is This For?

- If you've launched your first ad, but you're not getting the results you wanted.
- If you've created ads that worked before, but they're no longer working right now.

## What Does It Mean If Your Ad Is Not Working?

For most people, this means they're ads are not profitable.

So in this manual, I won't be focusing on Facebook Ads alone because there are other factors why your campaign is not profitable including your offer, funnel, traffic, or not knowing your customer lifetime value.

## 4 Ways To Fix An Unprofitable Campaign

### 1) Creating An Irresistible Offer

This is the mother of all conversions. Bad offers equal to bad results. Crazy offers equal to crazy results.

**4 Strategies to create an Irresistible Offer:**

- **Want vs Need** - You should know the difference between want and needs of your prospects/clients before you sell it to them. The key is to sell them what they want but give them what they need.
- **Blue Ocean Strategy** - This is a strategy to create an offer that is unique to you, it can be the same offer but you can make it unique by actually offering more bonuses.
- **"I'm Crazy" test** - This is a test to determine if it's irresistible. Are people saying that you're crazy for selling what you have to offer?
- **Dialogue Copy** - This is the words you use to present your offer.

### 2) Building A Converting Funnel

This is your system or your process that converts a stranger into a raving fan. This can be conducted online or offline.

**3 Strategies to Create a Converting Funnel:**

- **Funnel Structure** - What is your step by step process from start to finish? Are you modeling a structure that works or are you inventing your own way?
- **Funnel Design** - Even if you're modeling a successful funnel, you might not get the same results if you're also not modeling a successful design. Designs affect the visitor's emotion and perception.
- **Funnel Copy** - Copy is as important as the structure. This convinces people to go through the next step of the funnel. Without this, the structure and the design becomes useless.

### 3) Driving Quality Traffic

This is your process of acquiring leads or prospects. Are your leads quality enough? Do they have the willingness to buy your offer? Do they have the capacity and means to buy your offer?

**4 Strategies to Acquire Quality Traffic:**

- **Objective** - Facebook wants to give you the highest results possible. You can only do this if you tell Facebook your specific objective on running ads.
- **Targeting** - Are you showing your ads to the right audience? People who are willing and able.
- **Ad Creative** - Are you creating multiple ad creatives? Are you testing different angles, different copies, different backgrounds? This is to avoid ad fatigue.
- **Ad Copy** - Are you capturing the first 3 seconds of a viewer? Are you focusing your attention on your headline and first line text?

### 4) Increasing Customer Lifetime Value

The #1 reason why people stop running ads is because they think they are no longer profitable. However, they just didn't know their numbers ahead of time. Customer lifetime value is an essential metric that represents the total amount of money a customer is expected to spend in your business or on your products during their lifetime.

If your LTV is higher than your ad cost, you're certainly making money.

- **Increase Average Transaction** - Offering other products after the initial purchase increases the average transaction of each customer. This can be done through upsells, downsell, order bumps, and many more.
- **Increase Frequency of Purchase** - Even if you have only one product, but your product can be bought repetitively, you can increase your customer lifetime value. This usually applies to subscriptions like membership sites, physical products like clothes, and consumable products like supplements.
