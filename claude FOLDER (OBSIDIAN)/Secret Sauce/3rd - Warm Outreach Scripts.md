# Warm Outreach Scripts

> Part of the [[00 - Secret Sauce Index|Secret Sauce]] course. See also [[Sales MOC]] · [[Marketing MOC]].

## Disclaimer

This is just a rough outline of a sales script intended to give you a starting point so you can make your script.

> **Setter**: Hello, (Name)?
>
> **Prospect**: Yes, this is (Name).
>
> **Setter**: Hey (Name), si (Your Name) ito from COMPANY. Kumusta ka?
>
> **Prospect**: Okay naman, how about you?
>
> **Setter**: Doing great!
>
> Ako yung coordinator dito sa COMPANY, and I'm just calling kasi you responded to [an email, LinkedIn message, etc. (insert referral source)] from us about possibly helping you with your (problem you solve). Okay lang ba if we talk right now?
>
> **Prospect**:
>
> **IF YES**: Sige, I understand, mabilis lang 'to (positive tone).
> **IF NO**: *Continue with script*
>
> **Setter**: Perfect! So the purpose of this call is really to know more about your company, kung nasaan kayo with your (goal), and then share din how our process works to see if we can help.
>
> **Setter**: So, with that being said, nag-research na ako ng konti about (company), and I'm actually on your website right now. Mukhang you're doing some really great work in the [niche] space. I have a general understanding of what you do, pero in your own words, puwede mo ba akong bigyan ng mabilis na 20-30 second high-level overview of what you guys do and what makes you unique?
>
> **Prospect**: *summary of what they do*
>
> **Setter**: Wow, that's exciting to hear! (Insert custom compliment here)
> So, (name), was there something specific that piqued your interest kaya ka nag-respond sa email namin?
>
> **Prospect**: *Here's why I think I need your help*
>
> **Setter**: Awesome! So it sounds like it's good timing na nakapag-usap tayo ngayon! By the way, have you (step to reach goal, ex. Have you started to run ads) yet? *(wait for answer)*
> Ok, gotcha. So far, ano na yung nagawa niyo?
>
> **Prospect**: Yeah, I've done XYZ, or no I haven't because XYZ.
>
> **Setter**: *(if they've started to make progress, compliment them since it's not easy)*
> Wow, impressive! Hindi madali yung ginawa niyo. Ok, zooming out lang, what's the big goal ba for your company?
>
> **Prospect**: *BIG GOAL, THEY ARE PAINTING THEIR VISION*
>
> **Setter**: Great! So your goal is (repeat goal). How soon are you looking to achieve this?
>
> **Prospect**: I need to x by x date, or eh, I don't really need to x but it would help. *(Listen to what it would help with, if they don't tell you, ask – this is the lever we can pull to motivate them to take action).*
>
> **Setter**: Ok, got it. So, let me recap — right now, you're at (recap current situation), and ang goal mo is to (recap their goal) by (X date), and currently, you're doing (what they are doing now). Mukhang things are going fairly well… so, what made you reach out ulit?
>
> **Prospect**: *Highlight their pain points*
>
> **Setter**: Alam ko mahirap yan, and I really appreciate you sharing this with me. It sounds like this could be a good fit, and sa tingin ko, we should be able to help you out.
> Ok lang ba if I share my thoughts?
>
> **Prospect**: That'd be great!
>
> **Setter**: Great! So, we see this all the time. Based on what you just told me, ang madalas naming nakikita na nangyayari is (briefly overview why what they're doing isn't working).
>
> For example, since you have a low-ticket offer, hindi kayo nakaka-collect ng enough cash on day one, which, tulad ng sinabi mo kanina, you spend thousands on ads per month and don't see a positive return. Kaya if we put in an irresistible offer... like we did for XYZ na may similar business sa inyo, what would your business look like if we doubled the front-end cash collected? Mas makakagawa tayo ng mas malaking kita, di ba? Haha.
>
> **Prospect**: Yeah, that's reasonable.
>
> **Setter**: Cool! So, I actually have an entire video na nagpapakita exactly how to do this. May dalawang options: pwede natin siyang panoorin together sa next call natin, or I can send it over so you can watch it in between. Which do you prefer?
>
> **Prospect**: *They will take ownership of watching the video, which increases the likelihood they'll watch.*
>
> **Setter**: Great! Well, whichever works best for you. I have a slot at (X) bukas or (X) the day after. Which one works for you? *Set time within 36 hours*
>
> **Setter**: Real quick lang habang nagno-notes ako — ano nga ulit yung title mo sa [Company]? Ikaw ba ang founder?
>
> **Prospect**: I'm the founder.
>
> **Setter**: Awesome! And is there anyone else na sa tingin mo beneficial na kasama natin sa call? Like, may co-founders ka ba? We'd love to have them on the call too, kasi dahil sa nature ng ginagawa namin, we prefer working with all the founders.
>
> **Prospect**: Yes, gusto kong isama yung business partner ko.
>
> **Setter**: Perfect! Ano yung email nila? I'll go ahead and add them to the invite.
>
> **Prospect**: xyx@xyx.com.
>
> **Setter**: Great speaking with you! Sinend ko na yung link sa invite. Nakuha mo ba?
>
> *(wait for response)*
>
> Perfect! And the video — na-receive mo na ba?
>
> *(wait for response)*
>
> And last thing, sobrang puno na yung calendars namin, so is there any reason why hindi kita makikita sa (X time, X day)?
>
> **Prospect**: I'll be there!
>
> **Setter**: Perfect, talk soon! Have a great day!
