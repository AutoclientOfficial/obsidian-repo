>
> 4. The main pain point my marketing avatar is experiencing is **[enter it here]**

## Step #3: Customize

- **Step 3: Customize to Fit Your Brand Voice**

You can use the following prompts to customize the initial ad script:

**Simplify for a 5th Grader:**
- "Rewrite this text so that a 5th grader can easily understand it. Keep the language simple and the ideas clear."

**Bilingual Translation:**
- "Translate this text into a mix of 50% English and 50% Tagalog, making sure to retain the original meaning and keep it engaging."

**Add Intrigue and Curiosity:**
- "Modify this text to include elements of intrigue and curiosity. Add questions or hints that make the reader want to learn more."

**Include a Strong Call to Action:**
- "Enhance this text by adding a compelling call to action. Encourage the reader to take immediate steps, such as signing up, clicking a link, or contacting you."

**Make it Conversational:**
- "Revise this text to have a more conversational tone. Write as if you're speaking directly to the reader in a friendly and approachable manner."
