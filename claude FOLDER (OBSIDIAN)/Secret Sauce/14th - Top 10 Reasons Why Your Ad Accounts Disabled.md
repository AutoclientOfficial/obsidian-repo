# Top 10 Reasons Why Your Ad Accounts Disabled

> Part of the [[00 - Secret Sauce Index|Secret Sauce]] course. See also [[Sales MOC]] · [[Marketing MOC]].

### 1) Violation of the Facebook Ads Policies

- Using misleading content
- Advertising get rich quick opportunities
- Using claims, guarantees or promises
- Using sexual, sensational or disruptive content
- Selling illegal products and services

### 2) Creating an Ad Account in a New Facebook Account

### 3) Not Having A 2-Factor Authentication

- Two security methods options:
- Login codes from a third party authentication app.
- Text message (SMS) codes from your mobile phone.

### 4) Circumventing Facebook Policy

- Ads must not use tactics intended to circumvent our ad review process or other enforcement systems. This includes techniques that attempt to disguise the ad's content or destination page.

### 5) No Usage For More Than 60 Days

- What you're gonna see in your account: *"This account is more than 60 days old with little or no activity. We disable unused accounts to help prevent abuse and fraud."*

### 6) Payment Issues

- Like using the same credit card or payment details from a previously banned account, unsettled, or pending settlement. Avoid letting your ad account experience billing errors as this might give an impression to Facebook that you're not paying your dues on time.

### 7) Getting Multiple Disapproved Ads In A New Ad Account

### 8) Logging in to Facebook or your Ads Manager from multiple locations (different IP's)

- Every profile and Ad Account is connected to your main IP address. If Facebook sees multiple logins from different IP addresses from different locations, it flags the account as potential fraud and, sooner than later, Facebook will disable your Ad Account.

### 9) Collecting Disapproved Ads

- Make it a practice to delete any ads that have not been approved. As previously mentioned, having disapproved ads in your account can/does play a role in your new ads getting approved, and having too many disapproved ads can cause an account shutdown.

### 10) Spending Too Much Too Quickly

- Facebook is more strict to new ad accounts. If you have a new ad account it's important to "warm up" your ad account first before being aggressive.
