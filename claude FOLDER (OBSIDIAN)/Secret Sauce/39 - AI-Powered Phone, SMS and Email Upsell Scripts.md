# AI-Powered Phone, SMS and Email Upsell Scripts

> Part of the [[00 - Secret Sauce Index|Secret Sauce]] course. See also [[Sales MOC]] · [[Marketing MOC]].

## Step #1: Compile

**Gather Information for Upsell Scripts**

**PROMPT**

> "I need to create upsell scripts for Phone, SMS, and Email that will effectively increase the value of our current offer. To do this, please provide me with a detailed overview of our current product/service and the upsell offers.
>
> My current **[service/product]** is designed to help **[your target audience]** **[achieve X/eliminate Y]**.
>
> The main benefit of my **[service/product]** is **[enter it here]**.
>
> The main problem my **[service/product]** solves for my marketing avatar is **[enter it here]**.
>
> The main pain point my marketing avatar is experiencing is **[enter it here].**
>
> The upsell offer is **[describe upsell offer].**
>
> The unique value proposition of the upsell is **[enter it here]**.
>
> Some key features of the upsell are **[enter it here].**
>
> Please provide any additional information that could enhance the upsell scripts, such as specific pain points, objections, or customer testimonials related to the upsell offer."

## Step #2: Compose

**Draft Initial Upsell Scripts**

Using the information gathered, create initial drafts for each type of upsell script:

### Phone Script

**PROMPT**

> "I want you to write a persuasive phone upsell script that uses the following formula:
>
> - **Hook**: Grab attention with a strong statement or question.
> - **Offer Introduction**: Introduce the upsell offer and its main benefits.
> - **Value**: Explain how the upsell adds additional value to the current product/service.
> - **Objections Handling**: Address common objections or concerns.
> - **Call to Action**: Encourage immediate action.
>
> Use the information provided to craft a compelling phone script."

### SMS Script

**PROMPT**

> "Create a concise and persuasive SMS upsell script using the following structure:
>
> - **Attention**: Start with a hook to capture interest.
> - **Offer Brief**: Mention the upsell offer and its key benefits.
> - **Value**: Highlight the added value of the upsell.
> - **CTA**: Include a clear call to action with a link or instruction.
>
> Ensure the message is brief and engaging, fitting the SMS format."

### Email Script

**PROMPT**

> "Draft an engaging email upsell script with the following elements:
>
> Subject Line: Create a compelling subject line to increase open rates.
>
> - **Introduction**: Open with a hook that captures attention.
> - **Offer Presentation**: Introduce the upsell offer and explain its benefits.
> - **Value Proposition**: Detail how the upsell enhances the current product/service.
> - **Objections Handling**: Address potential objections or concerns.
> - **Call to Action**: Include a strong CTA encouraging the recipient to take action.
>
> Make sure the email is persuasive and aligns with our brand's voice."

## Step #3: Customize

**Customize the script as you see fit**

Customize the script and make it more conversational so that your audience will not notice that your closers are reading a script or you are sending them a templated script.
