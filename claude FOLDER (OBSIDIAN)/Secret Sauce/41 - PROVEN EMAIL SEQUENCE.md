# Proven Email Sequence

> Part of the [[00 - Secret Sauce Index|Secret Sauce]] course. See also [[Sales MOC]] · [[Marketing MOC]].

These email templates can be used anytime. These are perfect for broadcast emails may it be as a nurture email, post-purchase email and the like to continuously build relationships after your customer has bought from you.

## Email 1: Value Content 1

**Subject: The Shocking TRUTH about [Topic]**

> Alam mo ba?
>
> **[Share a unique fact on the challenges of your target market]**
>
> Ito ang masaklap..
>
> **[Share a shocking truth on what most people do when it comes to TOPIC]**
>
> Meaning, **[conclude the struggles of your target audience]**
>
> This will lead to:
>
> - **[Problem 1]**
> - **[Problem 2]**
> - **[Problem 3]**
>
> **If nakakarelate ka nito..**
>
> ..then ito ang pinaka-importanteng video na dapat mong panoorin ngayon!
>
> Dahil sa video na ito, ipapakita ko sayo ang isang **[Unique Mechanism]** to solve **[Major Problem]**..
>
> Tulad ng:
>
> - [Problem 1]
> - [Problem 2]
> - [Problem 3]
>
> Na hindi mo kailangan:
>
> - *[Objections or Hard Part 1]*
> - *[Objections or Hard Part 2]*
> - *[Objections or Hard Part 3]*
>
> **Kaya go ahead, watch this video now!**
>
> *- [Name of Product Owner]*
