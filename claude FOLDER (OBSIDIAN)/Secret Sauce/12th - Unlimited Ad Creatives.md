# Unlimited Ad Creatives: Crafting Engaging Ads to Capture Attention and Drive Results

> Part of the [[00 - Secret Sauce Index|Secret Sauce]] course. See also [[Sales MOC]] · [[Marketing MOC]].

In this guide, we explore various types of ad creatives that can elevate your paid advertising campaigns. Each ad type is designed to capture attention and engage your audience in unique ways.

Here you'll discover how to create:

- **Ad Copy**: Learn how to write compelling and persuasive ad copy that highlights the key benefits and drives action.
- **Event Video Ads**: Discover how to create dynamic video ads that promote events and generate excitement.
- **Image Ads**: Explore the power of striking images to make your ads visually appealing and memorable.
- **Testimonial Ads**: Utilize customer testimonials to build trust and credibility with potential leads.
- **Influencer Ads**: Leverage the reach and influence of key personalities to boost your brand's visibility and appeal.

This will guide you through crafting diverse and effective ad creatives that resonate with your audience and achieve your advertising goals.

## Ad Copy

The Ad Copy is the Text on your Ad Creative. You can use the AIDA or PAS Formula in writing an Ad Copy.

### Template: AIDA Formula

**ATTENTION**: Call out the Attention of Target Market

> Sample:
> Are you a business owner who ______?
> ATTENTION: Para sa mga negosyante na pagod na _______!
> Alam mo ba na 90% of businesses fail in the first 5 years?
> Are you part of the 10%?

**INTEREST**: State a Problem that Relates with Them or a Story

> Sample:
> Most business have these problems:

**DESIRE**: What they're gonna get with our workshop/program

> Sample:
> If ikaw to, then this is for you..
> I'll be conducting a _______..
>
> In this ________, you will discover:
> Benefit 1:
> Benefit 2:
> Benefit 3:

**ACTION**: Call-to-action

> Sample:
> Secure your spot now: LINK
> Go over this link to register now: LINK

## Image Ads

- Are creatives using Images only + Copy on the image.
- Hook them with the image ad and make them read the Ad Copy.

### Image Ads Checklist

**Copy**
- Are you using AI to propose compelling headlines and hooks for you?
- Are you using a problem-based hook?
- Are you using a desire-based hook?
- Are you using a go-for-the-kill or direct-to-the-point approach?
- Are you showcasing the Unique Selling Proposition (USP)?
- Are you testing calling out to your target market?
- Are you testing calling out a segment of your target market?

**Branding**
- Is Coach Fibo's face in the creative?
- Are you testing the same creative without Coach Fibo's face?
- Are you using actual photos (not stock photos)?
- Are you showing the actual product in the image? (if applicable)
- Are you showcasing a screenshot testimonial of the product/event?

**Visual**
- Is the image attention-grabbing?
- Is the text visible and readable?
- Are there fewer texts or is the creative full of text and difficult to read?
- Are you testing out-of-the-ordinary creativity?

## Testimonial Ads

Testimonial ads leverage the power of satisfied customers to build trust and credibility. By showcasing real feedback and success stories from your users, these ads highlight the value of your product or service through authentic, relatable experiences.

Testimonial ads can be in the form of written reviews, video testimonials, or quotes, and they serve to reassure potential customers by demonstrating how others have benefited from your offer.

This approach not only enhances your brand's credibility but also encourages new leads to take action based on proven results.

## Influencer Ads

Influencer ads involve partnering with trusted personalities to promote your brand. Here's how it's done:

1. **Approach**: Reach out to influencers who align with your brand values and have previously collaborated with you.
2. **Content Creation**: Have the influencer record a short, engaging video that highlights their positive experience with your product or service.
3. **Versatile CTA**: Ensure the call to action in the video is general, allowing the content to be reused for various offers and programs.

By leveraging the influence of these personalities, you tap into their audience, building trust and driving engagement with your brand across multiple campaigns.

Examples of influencer partnerships used:
1. Eduard Reformina — one of the top online marketing gurus in the Philippines.
2. Karl Serrano — one of the top network marketing influencers in the Philippines.
3. Real Talk Darbs — top content creation expert in the Philippines today.

With their millions of followers, this approach taps into their audience, leverages their authority, and piggybacks on their influence to gain trust with their followers and bring them to the programs and services being promoted.
