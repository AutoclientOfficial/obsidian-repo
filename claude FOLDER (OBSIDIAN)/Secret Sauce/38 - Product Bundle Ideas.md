# Product Bundle Ideas

> Part of the [[00 - Secret Sauce Index|Secret Sauce]] course. See also [[Sales MOC]] · [[Marketing MOC]].

## 1) Objection Breaking Bundle

This bundle strategy is very useful if you're selling a product that needs a presentation to sell (Ex: network marketing, insurance, courses, etc.)

These types of industries are usually harder to sell to a cold audience that's why you need a presentation to communicate the benefits of the product.

Even if your presentation is perfect, there will always be objections that stop your audience from buying.

Some example objections in the network marketing industry are:

- No experience
- Don't know how to sell
- Don't want to get rejected

In the insurance industry, here are a few objections your prospects might have:

- This is not my priority
- I might not be able to sustain my monthly payments
- I'm still young

So here's how you can use the objection breaking bundle... You create a bonus or an offer (usually digital product) that answers or solves the objection.

For example...

If your prospect wants to do network marketing however, he is afraid of recruiting his family and friends and doesn't want to get rejected…

You can bundle it with a FB ads course or FB ad templates so that they can copy & implement so that they can get downlines without having to recruit their family and friends.

Another example is if in the insurance industry, your prospect has the objection of like *"I only have 1 income stream, I don't know if I'll be able to sustain my monthly payments"*...

You can create a "10 Extra Income Idea Cheatsheet" where you interview your friends that are making money from video editing, freelancing, etc. and document how they're doing it.

So if your prospect will say... "I only have limited funds"...

You can say, I'll give you my 10 Extra Income Idea Cheatsheet! This will help you find extra income from 10 different income sources within 30 days so that you won't worry about losing your income source and so that you can increase your income.

The goal here is to create an offer where your prospects have no other objections why they cannot buy your product because you've solved it already for them.

## 2) Season Based Bundle

Almost every month, there's a season or an event... Like valentines season, summer season, rainy season, christmas season, etc.

This is just bundling or combining products that fit the specific season.

For example, if you're targeting to sell during the summer season... You can bundle the following:

- Sun glasses
- Cap
- Slippers
- Sando
- Sling Bag

If you're selling during the rainy season... You can bundle the following:

- Rain coat
- Rain shoe cover
- Foldable Umbrella

If you noticed, it's based on a theme or season.

This is very useful if you're doing ecommerce and buy products from China or Divisoria then selling it online.

Instead of selling just 1 product, you package multiple items to increase the perceived value.

## 3) More Products Bundle

This type of bundle is just letting your customers buy more of the same thing. This is very useful if you're selling consumables like supplements, skin care, make-up, Etc.

Things that people purchase monthly or regularly.

An example would be like…

- Buy 1 - P1,990
- Buy 2 + Get 1 FREE
- Buy 3 + Get 2 FREE

Or another angle is…

- Buy 1 - P1,990 each
- Buy 2 - P1,790 each
- Buy 3 - P1,590 each

The goal is to make them purchase more of the same thing so that we can increase our "Average Order Value"

Higher AOV means you have more financial capacity to spend on advertising.

## 4) Complimentary Product Bundle

This bundle strategy is finding similar products to combine with your core offer to make it irresistible.

Here are a few examples....

- Buy 3 boxes of coffee + Get 1 FREE Mug
- Buy 1 Rejuvenating Set + Get 1 FREE Serum
- Toothpaste + Toothbrush
- Ab Workout Tool + Dumbbells + Bench Chair
- Computer Set + Speakers + Webcam + Microphone

The goal of this bundle is that instead of them buying each item separately, all the things they need to achieve the desired result are already present in your offer.

## 5) Break Rubix Cube Bundle

Now this bundle strategy is one of the easiest to do which creates a huge difference but without increasing your costs.

Why? Because we're just breaking down your core offer into little parts so it looks irresistible.

It looks like this...

*(Illustration: "Current Service" shown as a whole Rubik's cube → "Superior Offer" shown as the same cube broken into its individual colored pieces)*

In this strategy, we increase the perceived value of your product through thin air. That means, we're just using the power of communication and perspective here.

Here's an example of one of my supplement products...

**What You're Gonna Get:**

- **FREE VIP Loyalty Membership** *(100 Members Only)*
- **50% Lifetime Discount** *(Save P2,000 Monthly)*
- **Fresh Shipment Every 30 Days** *(So You Don't Run Out)*
- **Pause Auto-Shipment Anytime** *(No Commitment)*
- **FREE Shipping** *(Anywhere in the Philippines)*
- **Cash On Delivery** *(Order Now Pay Later)*
- **Out-of-Stock Protection Guarantee** *(No Waiting)*
- **30-Day Money Back Guarantee** *(Peace of Mind)*

In the illustration above, I'm just selling 1 thing. It's a bottle of supplement. That's really it.

However, I communicated it with my audience differently. I made the offer "sexy"!

Aside from getting the supplement, I'm also sharing with them the services they're getting when they buy from us.

Because of this value stack, it made our product different and our customers cannot compare us with other products that are cheaper.

You cannot compare an apple to an orange right?

Instead of being an apple to apple comparison, we became an orange so that you can't compare us.
