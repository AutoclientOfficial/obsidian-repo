## Understanding the Sales Process

A well-defined sales process is the backbone of any successful business. It's the systematic journey that guides prospects from their initial contact with your brand all the way to closing the deal.

Understanding and optimizing this process is crucial for maximizing efficiency, ensuring consistency, and ultimately driving more sales. By mastering the sales process, you can identify gaps, refine strategies, and create a seamless experience that converts prospects into loyal customers.

### Steps

To streamline your sales process and improve efficiency, follow these detailed steps:

1. **Lead Generation:** Identify and attract potential customers who fit your target market.
2. **Lead Qualification:** Evaluate and prioritize leads based on their likelihood to convert.
3. **Needs Analysis:** Engage with leads to understand their needs, pain points, and goals.
4. **Presentation:** Deliver a tailored presentation or demo that addresses the lead's specific needs.
5. **Handling Objections:** Address any concerns or objections the lead may have, providing clear and convincing responses.
6. **Closing the Deal:** Finalize the sale by securing a commitment from the customer.
7. **Follow-Up:** After the sale, continue to engage with the customer to build relationships and encourage repeat business.

But not all sales processes or sales funnels are created equal. Using Russel Brunson's Dotcom Secrets Book, we were able to come up with Fibo's Marketing Flowchart.

## Low Ticket Funnels

Here are a few examples of low-ticket funnels:

### 1) Free + Shipping Funnel

- **Offer:** A physical book, report, or product that is free, with the customer only paying for shipping.
- **Example:** A fitness guidebook or a small gadget that normally retails for P999, offered for free + P250-500 shipping fee.
- **Funnel Steps:**
  - **Landing Page:** Offer the free product and collect shipping details.
  - **Order Bump:** Offer a small add-on product (e.g., an eBook or accessory) for P500-P1,000.
  - **Upsell:** After the initial purchase, offer a more comprehensive product or service, like a P2,500 - P5,000 course or toolkit.

### 2) Tripwire Funnel

- **Offer:** A low-cost product designed to convert leads into customers quickly.
- **Example:** A P299 video training or a P499 software tool.
- **Funnel Steps:**
  - **Landing Page:** Present the low-cost offer and emphasize its value.
  - **Order Bump:** Offer a related product for an additional P999.
  - **Upsell:** After purchase, offer a more advanced version of the product or a related service for P1,499 - P4,999

### 3) SLO (Self-Liquidating Offer) Funnel

- **Offer:** A low-cost product aimed at covering the cost of acquiring a customer.
- **Example:** A P1,499 workshop or a P999 toolkit.
- **Funnel Steps:**
  - **Landing Page:** Offer the low-cost product with a clear value proposition.
  - **Order Bump:** Add a related checklist, template, or resource for P499-P999.
  - **Upsell:** Offer a complementary product or service, such as a P2,499 - P4,999 membership or course.

### 4) Mini-Course Funnel

- **Offer:** A short, actionable course that solves a specific problem.
- **Example:** A P1,499 "30-Day Social Media Content Plan" course.
- **Funnel Steps:**
  - **Landing Page:** Promote the mini-course with a clear outline of benefits.
  - **Order Bump:** Offer a bonus module or resource for an extra P999.
  - **Upsell:** Follow up with a more comprehensive course or coaching package priced at P4,999 - P9,999.

### 5) Digital Product Funnel

- **Offer:** A low-cost digital product like an eBook, template, or checklist.
- **Example:** A P500 "Complete Meal Planning Guide" or a P1,000 "Startup Legal Checklist."
- **Funnel Steps:**
  - **Landing Page:** Present the digital product with strong benefits and a compelling offer.
  - **Order Bump:** Offer a related digital asset or tool for an additional P499 - P1,499.
  - **Upsell:** Offer a bundle of digital products or a more detailed guide for P2,499 - P4,999.

These low-ticket funnels are designed to introduce prospects to your brand, build trust, and create an entry point for more valuable offers down the line.

## Mid Ticket Funnels

### 1) Webinar Funnel

- **Offer:** A mid-priced course, program, or service sold through a live or automated webinar.
- **Example:** A P14,999 online course on digital marketing strategies.
- **Funnel Steps:**
  - **Landing Page:** Invite prospects to register for a free webinar that teaches valuable content related to the paid offer.
  - **Webinar:** Deliver high-value content during the webinar and pitch the paid offer at the end.
  - **Order Bump:** During checkout, offer a workbook, guide, or additional resources for P2,499 - P4,999.
  - **Upsell:** After purchase, offer a private coaching session or a more advanced course for P25,000 - P50,000.

### 2) Challenge Funnel

- **Offer:** A 5-day or 7-day challenge that leads into a mid-priced offer.
- **Example:** A P10,000 "5-Day Fitness Transformation Challenge" that includes daily live training and a meal plan.
- **Funnel Steps:**
  - **Landing Page:** Promote the challenge and collect registrations for a nominal fee (P2,500 - P5,000).
  - **Challenge:** Provide daily value and build momentum towards the main offer.
  - **Main Offer:** At the end of the challenge, pitch a P25,000 program or course.
  - **Upsell:** Offer a premium membership or ongoing coaching for P35,000 - P50,000.

### 3) Product Launch Funnel

- **Offer:** A newly launched mid-ticket product or service.
- **Example:** A P15,000 software tool for entrepreneurs or a P35,000 advanced photography course.
- **Funnel Steps:**
  - **Pre-Launch Content:** Share valuable content (e.g., blog posts, videos) to build anticipation for the launch.
  - **Launch Webinar or Video Series:** Provide an in-depth look at the product/service and its benefits.
  - **Sales Page:** Direct prospects to a compelling sales page offering the product at P15,000 - P25,000.
  - **Order Bump:** Include a complementary tool, add-on, or resource for an additional P2,500 - P5,000.
  - **Upsell:** After purchase, offer a related service or advanced training for P25,000 - P50,000.

### 4) Membership Funnel

- **Offer:** A mid-ticket membership program that provides ongoing value.
- **Example:** A P2,499/month membership site offering exclusive training, templates, and community access.
- **Funnel Steps:**
  - **Landing Page:** Offer a free trial or a low-cost introductory period (e.g., P499 for the first month).
  - **Sales Page:** Pitch the full membership benefits and convert trial users to full members at P2,499 - P4,999 per month.
  - **Order Bump:** Include a one-time offer for a related product or resource for P2,500 - P5,000.
  - **Upsell:** Offer an annual membership option at a discounted rate or a VIP upgrade for P15,000 - P30,000.

These mid-ticket funnels are designed to nurture and qualify leads, providing substantial value before presenting a higher-priced offer, making it easier to convert prospects into paying customers.

## High-Ticket Funnels

High-ticket funnels are designed to sell premium products or services, typically priced at $1,000 or more. These funnels often involve more personalized engagement and longer nurturing sequences to build trust and demonstrate value.

Here are some examples:

### 1) High-Ticket Coaching Funnel

- **Offer:** A premium coaching program or mastermind group.
- **Example:** A P250,000 6-month business coaching program.
- **Funnel Steps:**
  - **Landing Page:** Offer a free, high-value resource (e.g., a webinar, eBook) related to the coaching topic.
  - **Webinar or Video Series:** Provide in-depth content that highlights your expertise and the results clients can achieve.
  - **Application Form:** Prospects fill out an application form to qualify for the program, adding an element of exclusivity.
  - **Consultation Call:** Conduct a one-on-one consultation call where you discuss the prospect's needs and offer the P250,000 coaching program.
  - **Follow-Up:** After the call, use email follow-ups to reinforce the value of the program and answer any lingering questions.

### 2) High-Ticket Service Funnel

- **Offer:** A done-for-you service package or consulting service.
- **Example:** A P500,000 full-service marketing package.
- **Funnel Steps:**
  - **Landing Page:** Present a case study or testimonial video showcasing the success of past clients.
  - **Free Consultation Offer:** Invite prospects to book a free consultation call to discuss their needs.
  - **Consultation Call:** During the call, offer a tailored solution and present the P500,000 service package.
  - **Proposal Follow-Up:** Send a detailed proposal after the call, outlining the services, timeline, and benefits.
  - **Close the Sale:** Use follow-up emails or calls to address any concerns and secure the agreement.

### 3) Event Funnel

- **Offer:** A high-ticket live event or seminar.
- **Example:** A P150,000 weekend business growth seminar.
- **Funnel Steps:**
  - **Landing Page:** Offer early bird pricing or bonuses for those who register early.
  - **Webinar or Preview Event:** Host a free online event to give a taste of what attendees can expect, highlighting the value and results they can achieve.
  - **Sales Page:** Direct prospects to a sales page that emphasizes the event's benefits, testimonials, and what makes it unique.
  - **Order Bump:** Offer an additional resource, like VIP seating or a post-event mastermind session, for an extra P25,000 - P50,000.
  - **Upsell:** After the event, pitch a related high-ticket program or mastermind group.

### 4) Masterclass Funnel

- **Offer:** A high-ticket masterclass or advanced training program.
- **Example:** A P100,000 8-week intensive training course.
- **Funnel Steps:**
  - **Landing Page:** Offer a free mini-course or masterclass preview that provides value and showcases the depth of the full program.
  - **Video Series:** Send a series of videos that build on each other, leading to the pitch for the full masterclass.
  - **Sales Page:** The sales page should include testimonials, detailed curriculum, and a strong call-to-action to enroll in the P100,000 program.
  - **Order Bump:** Include an optional workbook or bonus module for an additional P10,000.
  - **Upsell:** Offer one-on-one coaching or a private group mentorship as an upgrade for P250,000 - P500,000.

### 5) High-Ticket eCommerce Funnel

- **Offer:** A luxury or high-end physical product.
- **Example:** A P150,000 premium leather handbag or a P200,000 home gym system.
- **Funnel Steps:**
  - **Landing Page:** Feature a compelling video or high-quality images showcasing the product's craftsmanship and exclusivity.
  - **Product Demo or Live Q&A:** Invite potential customers to a live product demonstration or Q&A session to answer their questions and build trust.
  - **Sales Page:** Present the product with detailed descriptions, testimonials, and a strong emphasis on its unique value.
  - **Order Bump:** Offer a complementary product or accessory for an additional P15,000 - P25,000.
  - **Upsell:** After the purchase, offer an extended warranty, personalized engraving, or a related high-ticket product for P50,000+.

High-ticket funnels are all about building trust, providing value, and ensuring the prospect feels confident in making a significant investment. They typically involve more touchpoints, personalized communication, and higher perceived value compared to low or mid-ticket funnels.
