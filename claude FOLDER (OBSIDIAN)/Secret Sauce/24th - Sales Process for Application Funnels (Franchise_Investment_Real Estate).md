# Sales Process For Application Funnels

> Part of the [[00 - Secret Sauce Index|Secret Sauce]] course. See also [[Sales MOC]] · [[Marketing MOC]].

This system is what we use to help our clients who are in the franchise business. It's a 3-step process on how we attract leads, nurture them even before our presentation and close them over the phone.

We call this our Automated Franchise Selling System. However, while this system is specifically created for franchise owners, we have also implemented the same system for other high ticket offers such as Co Ownership Investment Opportunities and Real Estate and it still works!

So if you have a high-ticket offer, then this application funnel is for you.

## Goal

In order to build an effective and highly profitable automated franchise selling system, there are three main elements that need to be in place.

These are:

- **Traffic**: getting high quality and qualified prospects 24/7 without relying on traditional and slow marketing methods
- **Process**: an automated system that filters the ready-to-buy franchisees and automatically rejects the unqualified ones.
- **Offer**: have an irresistible offer that separates you from the competition and making your prospects beg you to take their money

### AFSS Gameplan (Automated Franchising Selling System)

Unlimited Leads → **[Process: Filter → Booking Calendar → Leads Nurturing]** → Irresistible Offer → Phone Call → Sale

- **TRAFFIC** (unlimited leads via ads)
- **PROCESS** (filter, booking calendar, leads nurturing)
- **OFFER** (irresistible offer)

And here's the exact strategy on how we implement this.

## Strategy

### STRATEGY 1: Acquire High Quality and Qualified Prospects 24/7 Without Relying on Traditional and Slow Marketing Methods

**STEP 1: TRAFFIC**

**OLD METHOD**

When it comes to getting franchisees, most business owners rely on slow and effective ways of getting prospects online.

- They do manual posting on social media.
- Posting on groups.
- Posting their Facebook pages, but getting on very few likes and engagement.

Worse, they get inquiries, pero hindi naman qualified.

Other franchise owners do the traditional approach:

- Doing flyering,
- word-of-mouth marketing,
- and manually looking for prospective franchisees.

And because they can not get qualified prospects, they allow their friends and families to franchise their business but in the end aren't generating the revenue they are looking for.

Not only are these methods slow and ineffective, it makes the business owner burnout and stressed financially and emotionally.

**NEW METHOD**

- Using this method, you'll have less effort yet scalable business.
- You'll get unlimited leads (not limited only to your own reach, network or location)
- And you'll get faster results.

However, we don't do what most businesses do when it comes to advertising.

Normally what they do is just pressing the "Boost Post" button.

This doesn't work!

Also, most business owners, they hire the best copywriters to write the ad script, hire the best videographer and editors to create high-production videos for their ads..

..and when it doesn't work, **you just lost a LOT of money**.

**OUR METHOD**

That's the reason why we created a system to create and produce unlimited creative ideas so that you won't run out of advertising creatives to run.

We call this our **ACE Method** or **Ad Creative Engine**.

Because what we discover is that nobody can predict your winning ad creative.

Social media nowadays are powered with AI and unfathomable algorithms na sila na mismo ang magttest which creative should be shown to your audience.

So instead of trying to make the best creative, we push multiple and massive ad creative and we let the algorithm do its job to bring us unlimited leads for your franchise business.

And this system has helped our clients generate hundreds of leads per day.

**However, here's the problem..**

..out of those hundreds of leads per day, how do we know who are qualified?

Of course, you don't want to talk to unqualified prospects and waste your time, right?

This is where strategy 2 comes in…

### STRATEGY 2: Build an Automated System that Filters the Ready-to-Buy Franchisees and Automatically Rejects the Unqualified Ones

**STEP 2: PROCESS**

**OLD METHOD**

When it comes to closing franchisees, most franchise owners are doing these manually.

They handle inquiries on their Facebook page. And karamihan dun puro hindi qualified.

It's either curious lang sila, o wala talaga silang pera.

Business owners end up wasting a lot of time entertaining these unqualified leads.

Also, their sales process is manual.

Meaning, clients need to visit their franchise and then they close them via face-to-face presentation.

They end up using salesy tactics, being pushy or manipulative just to close a sale.

**NEW METHOD**

NOT with the AFSS.

By creating an **automated sales process**, lahat ng leads will undergo the same system.

Kung saan they will **automatically be filtered** where only the qualified prospects will be the ones who will be **able to book a call** with your team.

And even before the call, your prospects will undergo a ***"leads nurturing system"*** where all their possible objections, misconceptions and doubts about your franchise business will be handled.

Meaning, during the call, they are already a "close deal" and you don't have to hard sell or be pushy to close the sales.

**OUR METHOD**

Here's how the AFSS would look like:

First, all prospects or leads that are generated with our Ad Creative Engine will first undergo a filtering system.

In this step they will have to answer a series of questions that will determine if the client is "willing" and "able" to avail your franchise.

If NOT, they will **automatically be rejected**.

However, if they qualify, they will proceed to the next steps - booking a schedule.

In this step, we will gather relevant information so that during the call, we know exactly what to ask and talk about to help them make an informed decision.

And lastly, after scheduling a call, they will undergo a **"Leads Nurturing."**

This process involves sending them a series of targeted, automated emails and video content designed to address any remaining doubts or concerns they may have. These materials will educate them about the benefits of your franchise, showcase testimonials from successful franchisees, and provide answers to common objections. By the time they get on the call, they will already be informed, excited, and ready to invest.

This means that during the call, they are already a "close deal," and you don't have to hard sell or be pushy to close the sale.

With the AFSS, you're not just saving time—you're also increasing the quality of your leads, ensuring that every call your team takes is with someone who is genuinely interested and financially capable of joining your franchise. This system allows you to scale your business efficiently while maintaining high conversion rates and a positive customer experience.

### STRATEGY 3: Having Irresistible Offer that Separates You from the Competition and Making your Prospects Beg You to Take their Money

**STEP 3: OFFER**

**OLD METHOD**

When it comes to getting franchisees, most franchise owners are focused on selling..

During the call we are selling them products, pinapakita yung mga certificates pero hindi natin napapansin na hindi pala interested yung client or they end up saying a lot of excuses.

Dahil dito minsan kinakailangan na mag hard selling hanggang makabayad sila during the call.

**NEW METHOD**

Not with AFSS.

We want to create an offer that is so good, that it is impossible to resist. To make your offer irresistible, we need to make sure that the value that they are receiving is **10x more than what they are paying**.

**OUR METHOD**

Other than the system that we will be providing, we created our own irresistible checklist.

Kung saan, we want to make sure na yung offer mo is totally different from the competition.

If yung offer mo ay unique, different and ten times ang value, the clients will beg you to take their money.
