# Getting Your First Customers With Warm Outreach

> Part of the [[00 - Secret Sauce Index|Secret Sauce]] course. See also [[Sales MOC]] · [[Marketing MOC]].

## What is Warm Outreach?

Warm outreach involves reaching out to contacts who already have some level of familiarity with you or your business. These are individuals who have previously interacted with your brand—whether through social media, email subscriptions, or past engagements. Unlike cold outreach, warm outreach leverages existing relationships to make the communication more personal and relevant.

## Why It's Effective

1. **Established Trust**: Your contacts are already familiar with your brand, which makes them more receptive to your message.
2. **Higher Engagement**: People who have previously interacted with you are more likely to engage with your outreach efforts, leading to higher response rates.
3. **Personalization**: You can tailor your message based on their past interactions, making it more relevant and compelling.

## Step-by-Step Guide

### 1) Identify Warm Leads

- **Compile a List**:
  - Gather a list of phone contacts, social media followers, and email subscribers who have shown interest in your business. Look for people who have liked your posts, opened your emails, or attended your events.

- **Segment Your Contacts**:
  - Categorize them based on their level of engagement. For example:
    - **High Engagement**: Contacts who frequently interact with your content.
    - **Moderate Engagement**: Contacts who occasionally engage.
    - **Low Engagement**: Contacts who have shown some interest but haven't interacted recently.

### 2) Craft Your Message

- **Examples of Personalized Messages**:
  - **For Social Media Followers**:
    > "Hi [Name], thanks for liking our recent post about [Topic]. I wanted to let you know about an exclusive offer we have this month. Let me know if you're interested!"
  - **For Email Subscribers**:
    > "Hello [Name], I saw that you downloaded our eBook on [Topic]. We're offering a free consultation this week to help you implement the strategies you've learned. Interested?"

- **Tips for Compelling Messaging**:
  - **Be Personal**: Use the recipient's name and reference their previous interactions.
  - **Be Relevant**: Address their specific interests or needs based on past engagement.
  - **Include a Clear Call-to-Action**: Clearly state what you want them to do next, whether it's scheduling a call, making a purchase, or attending an event.

## Outreach Execution

- **Best Practices**:
  - **SMS**: Keep messages concise and to the point. Example:
    > "Hi [Name], it's [Your Name] from [Your Business]. We have a special offer just for you—reply if you're interested!"
  - **Email**: Use a clear subject line and personalize the email body. Example:
    > "Exclusive Offer for Our Valued Subscriber, [Name]!"
  - **Direct Messaging**: Engage in a friendly tone and be clear about your intention. Example:
    > "Hey [Name], I noticed you've been following us. We have something exciting coming up—would you like to know more?"
  - **Follow-Up Strategies**:
    - **Timing**: Follow up within a few days if you haven't received a response.
    - **Frequency**: Limit follow-ups to avoid being intrusive. A good rule of thumb is two follow-ups, spaced a week apart.
    - **Tone**: Be polite and respectful in follow-ups. Example:
      > "Just checking in to see if you had a chance to review my previous message. Let me know if you have any questions!"
