## Messenger "Setter" Script

**Objective**: 10 min Warm Up Call (this is the entire point of this process - get on the phone)

### Golden Response Rules For EVERY Message

1) Acknowledge/Encourage Build Rapport with the first statement.

MAKE THEM FEEL GOOD.

2) Ask Next Question In Sales/Setter Sequence

**Example**:
1) Acknowledgement: Oh cool - my aunt is an accountant. You must be very detail oriented.
2) Question: Does sitting a lot of your day make it hard to work out? / Does working in an office makes it hard to eat healthy? / Does being around the kids all day make it hard to eat healthy?

### Strategy

- Open
- Relate/Rapport
- Transition to Fitness ASAP
- Clarify Goals
- Label With Problem
- Overview Past Pains
- Create Curiosity
- CALL THEM

### Open

> Hello there NAME - Kamusta diyan?

### Relate

**KEY**: Relate all points back to not having time to **[Dreams or Goals]**. That's it.

**Warm** *(People who follow you or engage in content or like your page or start to follow you IG)*

**EXAMPLE:**

> ..kita ko nag comment ka or liked my post (thank you!) but we haven't had a chance to connect..
>
> ..I noticed that you've been following me and we haven't yet connected. Kita ko mahilig ka pala sa mountain climbing. I did this once, and it was crazy.. Super active ka pa rin ba?
>
> ... saw that you just started following my page, thank you! I see you have (kids, dog), do they keep you busy?

**Cold** *(People you cold message who don't know you)*

POINT HERE IT TO RELATE AND SEEM LIKE A REAL PERSON, NOT CREEPY

**EXAMPLE**:

> ... see that you have kids, do they keep you busy?
>
> .. see that you have a dog, does he keep you busy?

### Transition

> What do you do for work?
> Does it keep you busy?

### Follow Up

> Oh wow does it make it hard to eat healthy?
> Oh wow does it make it hard to workout?

### Sales Conversation Begins

1) Acknowledge & Compliment
2) Then follow with the next question in sequence.

### Clarify Goal

> So ano yung ginagawa mo when it comes to fitness? Ano yung goals mo?

### Label With Problem

> Got it! So you're trying to lose a few pounds. Ano naman yung biggest struggle or challenge mo?

### Overview Pain

> Ano na yung na try mo before?
> How did that work for you? For how long?

### Create Curiosity

> Totally get it. Meron kasi ako mga clients din na katulad mo. I definitely have some suggestions for you (if gusto mo). I just like to ask before sharing some advice. Hehe

### Get On The Phone

If they have time to be messaging you, they have 5min to get on a call.

> Awesome! It would take me 12,423 pages to type out haha pero kung meron kang few minutes, we can hop on a quick call tapos explain ko sayo.. Would you be up for that? (no pressure)

**2 Options:**

**1) Call Them Right Now**

> Cool, here's my number 0999-XXX-XXXX what's yours?

**2) Set A Time** *(WAYY less preferable, it's a 5-minute call, you have to do it THAT DAY)*

*Remember: **TKAD. TIME KILLS ALL DEALS.***

> Cool. You can check out this link para pwede ka makapag schedule ng anong preferred mong time. Once meron ka ng napili, just let me know para ma confirm ko ito on my side.

IMPORTANT TO LEAVE AN OPEN LOOP.

### Follow Up

> Hey, any issues?
> Na notice ko na hindi ka pa nakapag schedule, everything good?

## Cold Email & DM Scripts

### Are You Still Looking For [4-Word Desire]?

No images. No frills. No links. Just a question. Nothing else. This message is money for getting leads to engage. And it's among the first things I do when I invest in a new business.

**Examples**

Are you still looking to

- ...buy your dream home?
- ...get more sales leads?
- ...tone up your arms?
- ...open an online store?
- ...start a youtube channel?
- ...build your personal brand?
- ...build a cleaning business?
- ...boost your SAT score?
- ...get more gym clients?
- ...boost your credit score?
- ...get that six pack?
- ...write your first book?
- ...buy your dream boat?

**Pro Tip**

Use "still" if you are reaching back out to a list. IF you are reaching out to a list cold (for the first time) remove the "still."

### Cold Email

> **Subject**: Are you open?
>
> Hey FIRSTNAME,
>
> Are you guys open right now?
>
> I can see your goals for your clients.
>
> We specialize in helping (Avatar) get (goal) in under (time) without (pain)
> I know that's a bold claim, but we've helped (x clients) in just under (time) generate over (results)
>
> Some of our clients include (notable clients)
>
> The best part is (irresistible offer)
>
> Does it make sense for us to hop on a quick 15 minute call to discuss further?
>
> Here is my link if so: BOOKING LINK
>
> Case Studies:
>
> CASE STUDY 1:
> LINK
>
> CASE STUDY 2:
> LINK
>
> *Note: Include 5-12 case studies*
>
> **Obviously you have a few options here...**
> 1) Do nothing and XYZ
> 2) Risk it and get paid (irresistible offer.)
>
> Click my booking link below, pay $1 to make $15k, see what else we have to offer.
> BOOKING LINK
>
> Look forward to speaking with you soon!
>
> P.S. just respond back "no" if you're not interested in receiving these emails again

### Respond to ALL POSITIVE REPLIES

> Hi [First Name],
>
> Thanks for your reply. Initially we find it best to have a quick 3 ½ minute chat to see
>
> Whether there's a good fit or not.
>
> Here is a link to my calendar where you can book a time to suit you:
> LINK
>
> I look forward to speaking with you very soon.
>
> Thank you in advance.

### Respond to Negative Replies

> Hey [FIRST NAME], so sorry to have bothered you, hope you enjoy your day
