# AI Market Research Prompts

> Part of the [[00 - Secret Sauce Index|Secret Sauce]] course. See also [[Sales MOC]] · [[Marketing MOC]].

### Prompt 1: Data Collection

Analyze the following text and tell me what the product is and who it's for.

Text to analyze:

> *[Insert Initial Details from Client or Product]*
>
> *Product Details may include, but not limited to:*
> - *Brand name*
> - *Problem it solves*
> - *Ideal target market*
> - *Ingredients (if applicable)*
> - *Features and Benefits*
> - *Uniqueness*
> - *Pricing*
> - *Guarantee*

### Prompt 2: Product

From now on, we will refer the term [PRODUCT] to the **[INSERT THE CLIENT'S PRODUCT HERE]**. Got it?

### Prompt 3: Role Play

You're a talented copywriter from agora with decades of experience selling [THE PRODUCT] and who has a keen sense of trends and an understanding of the market. Do you understand?

### Prompt 4: Top Target Market

What are some prospects that might purchase [PRODUCT]? List 10 possible prospects.

Who are the 20% of the people of this market who likely drive 80% of the sales of **[PRODUCT]**? List the top 3 most likely prospects and describe their likely profession. Please indicate the number one most likely and why you think so.

> *[IMPORTANT NOTE:*
>
> *Out of the Top 3 Target Market, you need to choose which ONE is the most probable market that we will focus on. The market who is the most willing and able to buy our products and services.*
>
> *Contact the Copy Chief if NOT sure who to choose.]*

### Prompt 5: Market

From now on, we will refer the term [TARGET MARKET] to the **[INSERT TOP TARGET MARKET]**. Got it?

### Prompt 6: Problems

Topic: **[PRODUCT]**

For the above topic, what are some common and uncommon problems **[TARGET MARKET]** experiences?

### Prompt 7: Pains

What are the pains [TARGET MARKET] might experience intensely if they don't solve these problems? Be specific, detailed, vivid, unique, uncommon, and give up to 5 examples.

### Prompt 8: Frustrations

What are the frustrations of [TARGET MARKET] might experience intensely if they don't solve these problems? Be specific, detailed, vivid, unique, uncommon, and give up to 5 examples.

### Prompt 9: Top Pain and Frustration

Of all the pains and frustration listed above and any others that you know, what do you think is the single biggest pain point that would cause them to take urgent action to solve it?

### Prompt 10: Fears

List 10 of their biggest fears and the one fear that keeps them up at night when they are not able to solve these problems, pains and frustrations.

### Prompt 11: Old Method or Possible Competition

What have the [TARGET MARKET] tried/used/bought to solve their problems. Give me 5 examples of each along with the following details:

- Name of Solution:
- Why did they tried this:
- Cost of the solution:
- Why they regret the solution:
- Emotional trauma failure caused:
- How does this affect their next purchase decision for a solution?

### Prompt 12: Objections

What are the prospect's 10 biggest objections about purchasing this product? Be specific and give detailed examples to each.

### Prompt 13: Goals

If [TARGET MARKET] solves those problems, relieves those pains, and alleviates those fears, what goals can someone finally achieve? Be specific, detailed, vivid, unique, uncommon, and give up to 5 examples.

### Prompt 14: Desires

If [TARGET MARKET] performs those actions and tasks, and achieves their goals, what kind of desires will they fulfill? Be specific, detailed, vivid, unique, uncommon, and give up to 5 examples.

### Prompt 15: Happiness

If [TARGET MARKET] performs those actions and tasks, and achieves their goals, what would happiness look like to them? Be specific, detailed, vivid, unique, uncommon, and give up to 5 examples.
