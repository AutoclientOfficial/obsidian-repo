# Account Disabled Recovery Script

> Part of the [[00 - Secret Sauce Index|Secret Sauce]] course. See also [[Sales MOC]] · [[Marketing MOC]].

## Ad Account Appeal

> Hello Facebook Team,
>
> My ad account was mistakenly disabled.
>
> As an advertiser, I understand that it is my responsibility to provide valuable content to the best interest of your users while complying with the advertising policies.
>
> I am in good faith in advertising to improve the experience of your users and to encourage them to use your platform more often.
>
> I am also in compliance with your advertising policies and I make sure that I follow your rules and standard.
>
> I want to continue to provide more value to my audience by extending my reach through advertising.
>
> Is it ok if you give my ad account a check?
>
> I would appreciate your kind response and action.
>
> Thank you so much :)
