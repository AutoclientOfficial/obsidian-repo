## Million Views Checklist

This is the checklist we use to ensure our content gets those million views and engages the audience:

- [ ] **Challenge Beliefs** *(Emotion)*
  - Shake up common ideas and make people feel strongly about your content.
- [ ] **Common Knowledge** *(Relatability)*
  - Use familiar topics that everyone can connect with.
- [ ] **Pick the Right Side** *(Engagement)*
  - Take a clear stance on an issue to encourage interaction and discussion.
- [ ] **Intense Title** *(Attention)*
  - Create a striking and interesting title that grabs people's attention.
- [ ] **Thought Provoking** *(Shareability)*
  - Offer new ideas or insights that make people want to share your content.

## Crafting Attention-Grabbing Titles and Descriptions

Here's how we craft our titles and post descriptions to grab attention and make an impact:

- Simple enough for a 4th grader to understand
- Build intrigue and self-interest
- Be bold & eye-catching
  - STOP _____
  - Before you do X, watch this
- Uncommon word combinations
  - Topic + Opposite of what most people think of it. (Blue Mango, Unemployed Millionaire, UTANG ang nagpayaman sa akin!)
- Dates in the title leads to 20% higher views
- Brackets and parentheses in the title boosts views by 30%

## Content Structure

*(Applicable to long, short videos and reels)*

- [ ] **HOOK**
  - QUESTION
    - That qualifies your audience
    - A question you know they will disagree
  - STORY
    - Start with a date
    - Start with something extraordinary
    - Start with the word "Imagine"
  - FACTS
    - Say something extraordinary
    - Say an interesting fact
- [ ] **STORY**
- [ ] **VALUE**
- [ ] **QUESTION** *(If applicable)*

## Sample Script

**TITLE 1:** Ang Katotohanan ng Insurance... Mag Ingat!
**TITLE 2:** Ang mga TOXIC marketing ng insurance agents. Wag magpaloko!

**HOOK**

**[Ask a question that qualifies your audience]**

> Nasayang na ba ang pera mo ng dahil sa insurance?

**[State at least 3 facts or common knowledge your audience can relate]**

> Ang sabi magagamit mo daw sa hospital, pero pagpresent mo nito, pinabayad ka pa rin ng downpayment.
>
> Ang sabi 10 years to pay lang, pero pagkalipas ng 10 years, pababayarin ka na naman.
>
> Ang sabi tutubo daw ang pera mo, ngayon negative ang tubo ng pera at ang laki pa ng charges na hindi naman sinabi ng agent mo.
>
> Bayad ka ng bayad, wala ka namang napapala.
>
> At nung may claim na, pahirapan sa daming requirements..
>
> Yung feeling mo tuloy, parang na scam ka lang..
>
> Ganito din ba ang experience mo sa insurance?

**[Ask a question that will challenge their beliefs or plant a doubt]**

> Well ngayon..
>
> ..malalaman mo na ang katotohanan sa likod ng insurance industry.
>
> At totoo ba talagang gusto kang tulungan ng financial adviser mo o habol lang ba niya ang commission sa pera mo?

**STORY**

**[Leverage other people's stories]**

> May nakita akong post sa social media na nakapag trigger talaga sa akin..
>
> ..sabi nito:
>
> *May pang gala pero walang pang insurance.*
>
> *May pang inom pero walang pang insurance.*
>
> *May pang mamahaling kape pero walang pang insurance.*
>
> *May pang handa sa 1 week bday, 1 month bday, 1 year bday, at kung ano ano pang handaan! Pero walang pang insurance.*
>
> *Mahal mo ba talaga ang pamilya mo?*
>
> Napatanong nalang ako:

>
> At ito yung worse..
>
> ..isa lang ito sa dami daming mga "toxic" marketing na ginagawa ng mga financial advisers.

> Yung iba tinatakot nila yung mga prospects.
>

>
> At yung masaklap, ginagamit ang pagkamatay ng ibang tao para lang makabenta ng insurance nila.

>
> At yan ang nakikita kong problema ngayon sa insurance industry.
>

>

>

**[Transition by asking a thought-provoking question or taking the other side]**

> Pero alam mo ba..
>
> ..hindi nila kasalanan ito.
>
> Dahil being a financial adviser it NOT an easy career.
>
> Lalong lalo na ngayon sa social media.
>

>

>

>

**[Ask a thought-provoking question..]**

> Kung ikaw yung nasa situation nila..
>
> ..yung ilang prospects na yung nakausap mo pero wala ka pa ring sales?
>
> Yung may quota kang dapat makuha or else mawawalan ka ng trabaho at may pamilya kang sinusuportahan?
>
> Do you think gagawin mo ang lahat para lang makabenta?

**[Give a sneak peek on your realization between what most people know and you think is the truth]**

> While I don't agree sa iilan na mga marketing tactics nila, what I want you to realize is that..
>
> ..tao din sila na nagtatrabaho para sa family nila.
>
> Kasi ito yung katotohanan ng insurance industry.

**VALUE**

**[Explain or show proof of your claim]**

> Ang insurance o ang financial industry as a whole...

>

>
> Pero nagffail sila, dahil hindi sila nakapag plano ng maayos.

> At ang financial industry ang solution para magawa mo ito.

**[Share closing remarks and what they should do]**

> Kaya make sure na piliin ng tama ang financial adviser mo.

>
> Dun mo malalaman na genuine yung kausap mo at hindi lang habol ang pera o commission nito.

**[Close the content by sharing a conclusion or ask a thought-provoking question]**

> So yes, the financial industry is the best place for you to grow your money and build wealth.
>
> Siguraduhin mo lang, na you spend time and effort in finding the best product, best company and best financial adviser for you.
>
> Because at the end of the day..
>
> ..no one is more interested in your financial success than you.
