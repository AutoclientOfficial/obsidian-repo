# Conversion Optimization Process

> Part of the [[00 - Secret Sauce Index|Secret Sauce]] course. See also [[Sales MOC]] · [[Marketing MOC]].

This is the method you use to turn more of your website visitors or leads into paying customers. It involves analyzing and improving various aspects of your sales funnel to increase the likelihood that visitors will take the desired action, such as making a purchase or signing up for a service. By refining your approach, you can boost your conversion rates and achieve better results from your marketing efforts.

## Funnel and Numbers

Funnel flow: Ads Spent → Ads (CPC) → Landing Page (LPCR) → Total Sales (# Buyers, # Sets)

### Raw Data:

- Ad Spent
- Total Sales
- Total Sets
- CPC

### Data to be Computed:

- CPP
- % COGS
- % Ads
- Gross Profit
- AOV
- Target Numbers:
  - ROAS: 5x
  - Ads + COGS = 40% of Revenue
  - Net Profit - 50% of Revolving Capital (Ads + COGS)

### Ideal Numbers:

- COGS: 20%
- Ads: 20%
- Logistics: 15%
- Overhead: 15%
- Profit: 30%
- TOTAL: 100%

## Conversion Optimization

Funnel flow: Ads Spent → Ads (CPC) → Landing Page (LPCR) → Total Sales (# Buyers, # Sets)

### Scale:

- Great *(> Target)*
- Meh *(= Target)*
- Bad *(< Target)*

Funnel Conversion and CPC = Great, IDEAL Scenario ✅
Conversion = Great, CPC = Meh, Optimize CPC
Conversion = Great, CPC = Bad, Optimize CPC
Conversion = Meh, CPC = Great, Optimize Conversion
Conversion = Meh, CPC = Meh, Optimize Both
Conversion = Meh, CPC = Bad, Check Product to Market Fit
Conversion = Bad, CPC = Great, Optimize Conversion
Conversion = Bad, CPC = Meh, Assess Product Viability
Conversion = Bad, CPC = Bad, Ditch Product

### CPP < Target

**Test Ad Creatives**

- Voice Over Creatives
- Testimonial-based video
- Testimonial-based image
- Focus on the Product and Unique Mechanism
- Problem-based creatives

**Test Target Market & Messaging**

- Target Problem-Aware
- Target Solution-Aware
- Target Product-Aware

### Conversion < Target and CPC > Target

- **Copy**
  - **Headline**
    - Who is the Target Market?
    - Awareness Level
      - Problem-Aware
      - Solution-Aware
      - Product-Aware
    - Market Sophistication
      - 1st Level (Basic Messaging)
      - 2nd Level (Bigger Claims)
      - 3rd Level (Add a Unique Mechanism)
      - 4th Level (Unique Mechanism + Bigger Claims)
      - 5th Level (Identification)
  - **Messaging**
    - What Problem are you Solving?
    - What is their Awareness Level
    - Are you using Bullets?
    - Are the Features and Benefits Clear?
    - Is there a Unique Mechanism?

- **User Interface**
  - **Is it Mobile Friendly?**
  - **Types of Funnels**
    - VSL
    - WSL
    - Messenger
  - **Theme**
    - Color theme
    - Premium theme
      - Black BG
      - Gold Theme
      - Minimalist *(ex. Apple)*

- **Pricing**
  - Increase Price
  - Add Bundling
  - Add Upsells
  - Promos or Discounts
  - Adding Scarcity
