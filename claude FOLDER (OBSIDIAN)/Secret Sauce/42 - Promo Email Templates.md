# Promo Email Templates

> Part of the [[00 - Secret Sauce Index|Secret Sauce]] course. See also [[Sales MOC]] · [[Marketing MOC]].

This email template is perfect if you want to give away promo or discounts for your products. What better way to encourage repeat orders is by rewarding your loyal customers a discount of your product.

## Email 1: We Miss you Email

*(To be sent to an email list that you haven't sent an email for a long time)*

**Subject: We've missed you!**

> Hi there..
>
> Greetings from **[Your COMPANY]**.
>
> Your brand partner in **[Your INDUSTRY]**.
>
> You are receiving this email, because you've purchased in the past our best-selling product:
>
> *[INSERT PRODUCT NAME HERE]*
>
> And yes, I know. For some of you, medyo matagal na since we sent you an email.
>
> That's why we miss you.
>
> Now we are sending you this email dahil…
>
> *[INSERT REASON WHY HERE. Example: Sale, Promo or exclusive announcement]*
>
> That's why this coming **[DATE]**, we will be giving:
>
> **[Name of Promo/Event/Sale Here]**
>
> ..for all our loyal customers.
>
> **And here's how it works:**
>
> *[INSERT PROMO MECHANICS HERE] Example: Buy 1 Get 1. Buy 1 Get a Mystery Gift*
>
> Woohoo! 🎉🥳
>
> So if you are planning to buy **[PRODUCT]** or haven't reordered for a while..
>
> ..this is your BEST chance.
>
> **So click here to place your order now**
>
> Warm Regards,
> **[COMPANY]**
