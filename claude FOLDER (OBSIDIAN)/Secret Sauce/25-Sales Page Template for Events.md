# Sales Page Template For Events

> Part of the [[00 - Secret Sauce Index|Secret Sauce]] course. See also [[Sales MOC]] · [[Marketing MOC]].

This template is for selling events, tools, or other low-ticket items that need some explaining.

The best part? The script you use for your video will be the same as the text on your sales page. This keeps your message consistent and makes it easier to explain your offer.

## [HEADLINE + SALES VIDEO]

Example:

> Para sa mga **negosyante** na palaging **stressed**, **kulang sa tulog**, at madalas na **b-burn out**..
>
> **17 AI Tools That Will *Duplicate Yourself*, *Replace Your Employees*, and Make Your Business *Run Without You*…**
>
> *(So That You Can FINALLY Keep More Profit in the Bank, Get Your Life Back, and Do What You Love Without Worrying About Money)*
>
> ☆ Watch video for full price details
>
> CLAIM 90% DISCOUNT » *First 1,000 Enrollees Only*

## [PROBLEM]

Ask questions that qualify your prospects. Normally, you ask questions about problems your target audience is currently facing.

Example:

> From: **Fibo Lim**
> Cebu City, Philippines
>
> **Dear Entrepreneurs & Business Owners,**
>
> Na **b-burn out** ka na ba sa business mo?
>
> Yung tipong, **hindi ka makatulog** gabi gabi dahil sa *dami mong iniisip*?
>
> Lagi ka bang **stressed** sa negosyo mo?
>
> Yung *wala kang time* mag *relax* at mag *enjoy* dahil laging ka nalang nag ooverthink sa **dami ng kailangan mong gawin**?
>
> Gusto mo bang makatakas sa *entrepreneurial rat race*? At finally magkaroon ng **time, money at freedom as an entrepreneur**?

## [SOLUTION]

Here share the solution or your BIG PROMISE to solve your target audience's problems.

Example:

> If YES..
>
> ..then this is the most important blog you need to read.
>
> Dahil mamaya, ipapakita ko sayo..
>
> ..ang solution to getting rid of *stress, sleepless nights and burn out!*
>
> At irereveal ko sayo itong **NEW way of making more money** in your business with *less time and effort*…
>
> - Without *increasing your overhead expenses*
> - Without *hiring more people*
> - And without *doing everything by yourself*
>
> Dahil malalaman mo kung paano mo ma *duplicate ang sarili* mo at finally *fire yung mga headache employees* mo!

## [AUTHORITY]

Before you share your product, you need to show your authority and credibility first. Share at least 3 reasons why they should listen to you.

Example:

> Hi! Ako nga pala si **Fibo Lim**...
>
> Unless you are living in a cave o hindi ka gumagamit ng *social media*, marahil kilala mo na ako..
>
> I currently **own multiple businesses** in *different industries*…
>
> Meron akong supplement business, ecommerce solution business, franchise brokerage business and a digital marketing business.
>
> At the same time, gumagawa ako ng content in multiple social media platforms!
>
> Kaya ang laging tanong nila sa akin ay:
>
> *"Paano mo ito nagagawa? Sa sobrang busy mo, hindi ka ba nabburn out? May time ka pa ba to relax and enjoy?"*

## [STORY]

To build trust with your target audience, share your story and show that you understand their struggles because you've faced similar challenges yourself.

Share your struggles, share your discovery of the solution, share your results and transformation.

Example:

> Just like you, when I got started, I **used to work 24/7** sa business ko…
>
> I can totally relate sa mga struggles ng mga business owners at entrepreneurs.
>
> Yung tipong ang **dami dami mo pang kailangan gawin**, tapos hindi mo makuha ng empleyado mo ang gusto mo.
>
> O minsan *incompetent* yung employee na na hire mo, na **you end up doing everything on your own**.
>
> O kung meron man magaling, ang *mahal ng sweldo o rates nila*.
>
> Yung at the end of the month, ang laki laki ng sales mo… Pero once you deduct the operating expenses and salaries…
>
> **Konti nalang yung natiring profit, o minsan break even… O baka negative ka pa!**
>
> Sobrang stressed ka na... *Wala kang time sa sarili mo* at *wala pang laman ang bank account mo*!
>
> Ang saklap diba?

## [OFFER]

Transition to the offer. Share the uniqueness of the offer and what problems it can solve.

Example:

> And how you wish…
>
> ..kung pwede lang sana, na ma duplicate mo yung sarili mo...
>
> - Para merong magtatrabaho na *hindi nagrereklamo*
> - Yung palaging andyan, *hindi nag a-absent at hindi nale-late*
> - At magkakaroon ka ng *time at freedom* as an entrepreneur
>
> Well, ngayon, *posible* na ito…
>
> **Introducing AI or artificial intelligence!**
>
> Nung na discover ko ito, I got so excited…
>
> Dahil finally, I can **fire yung mga headache employees** ko, ma **duplicate yung sarili ko** and **achieve more in less time**.
>
> Kaya, gumawa ako ng **personal collection of AI Tools** para magamit ko sa own businesses ko.
>
> At later, sasabihin ko sayo paano mo din ma a-access ito…
>
> Dahil if you are someone who wants to get rid of *stress, sleepless nights and burn-out*... Then para ito sayo!
>
> Kaya basahin mo itong mabuti because this… Might change your life forever!
>
> - Why pay for **writers** kung pwede naman itong gawin ng AI for **FREE**? Just type what you want, click a button and in seconds, may script ka na!
> - Why hire a **graphic designer**, kung pwede naman itong gawin ng AI with **zero cost**? Just type what you want, click a button, tapos in a flash, may image ka na!
> - Why pay for a **programmer**, kung kaya itong gawin ng AI **without you spending a single peso**? Again, Just type what you want, click a button, and it will be ready in seconds!
>
> Isn't that CRAZY?!

## [PRICE ANCHOR]

Now you want to anchor alternative solutions and other prices so that your audience has a benchmark to compare your product and or price.

Example:

> Sounds good?!
>
> Ngayon, you might be wondering:
>
> *"Magkano naman para ma access ang mga tools na ito?"*
>
> If you hire a **graphic designer**, it would easily cost you P30,000 to P60,000 per month!
>
> Kung **Facebook ads specialist** naman that's around P40,000 to P80,000 per month!
>
> Or if you hire an **experienced copywriter**, expect to be paying around P50,000 to P100,000 per month!
>
> Now obviously, I'm not going to charge you P100,000...
>
> Not even P50,000…
>
> Nor P20,000…

## [PRICE REVEAL]

Now mention the regular price. But justify the price by asking the "Is it Worth It?" questions:

Example:

> But today, you can get access to my personal collection of AI Tools for only a one-time payment of P9,999!
>
> Eto yung tanong ko sayo…
>
> - Kung ma **eliminate** mo yung mga *headache employees*, ma **duplicate** ang sarili mo *without hiring more people*... **Magkano ba yung pwede mong ma save sa business mo?**
> - Kung magkakaroon ka ng **more time** as an entrepreneur.. Yung hindi ka *stressed*... Walang *burn out*… At mas may *time* ka to *relax and enjoy* your life... **Magkano ba yung worth niyan sa'yo?**
> - Kung magkakaroon ka ng **freedom**... Yung *magttravel* ka *habang kumikita* ang business mo... Yung *mabibili mo lahat ng gusto mo na hindi pinoproblema ang pera*... At ma achieve mo lahat ng mga pinapangarap mo.. **Magkano ba yung worth niyan sa'yo?**
>
> **Do you agree that's worth more than P9,999 diba?**

## [SPECIAL PRICE REVEAL]

Reveal the special price and the reason why you are doing this. Add a Call-to-Action.

Example:

> Pero eto… May good news ako!
>
> Because hitting **500,000 followers on Facebook**, I want to **give back to my subscribers**!
>
> *Fibo Lim — Public Figure, 483K followers*
>
> That's why for a **limited time only, available only on this page**…
>
> Magbibigay ako ng **90% Discount Coupon** as a *"Thank You Gift"* sa inyo!
>
> Ibig sabihin, instead of paying our regular price of P9,999…
>
> You can get access to my **personal collection of AI tools** for **P999 ONLY!**
>
> **One-Time Payment and Lifetime Access!**
>
> All you have to do is click the button below and claim your 90% discount coupon!
>
> CLAIM 90% DISCOUNT » *First 1,000 Enrollees Only*

## [GUARANTEE]

Add a money back guarantee if it is applicable. And close the script.

Example:

> At dahil I always want to *overdeliver* and *make my clients happy*…
>
> Bukod pa sa 90% discount, I'm going to give you an **irresistible guarantee** para hindi mo na ito kailangan pag-isipan.
>
> Kung sa tingin mo *hindi ito nakatulong sa business mo* for whatever reason… **I'll refund 100% of your money back, no questions asked!**
>
> Sounds fair?
>
> Well, my friends tell me I'm *stupid & crazy* for giving a guarantee like this…
>
> Pero ganun ako ka confident na this will finally *eliminate your stress, sleepless nights, and burnout.*
>
> And will help you *gain back your time, money, and freedom* as an entrepreneur.
>
> So again, click the button below, and claim your 90% discount coupon!
>
> CLAIM 90% DISCOUNT » *First 1,000 Enrollees Only*
> *(with countdown timer)*
