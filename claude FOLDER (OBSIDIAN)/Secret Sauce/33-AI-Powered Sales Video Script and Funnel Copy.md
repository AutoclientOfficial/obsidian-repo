# How to Create AI-powered Sales Video Scripts and Funnel Copy

> Part of the [[00 - Secret Sauce Index|Secret Sauce]] course. See also [[Sales MOC]] · [[Marketing MOC]].

## Sales Video Script

### Compile

**Step 1: Do an AI-Powered Market Research**

Before writing a single word of copy, use AI to do a thorough and deep dive market research.

Disclaimer: Of course, nothing beats a thorough market research by asking your actual target audience and customers for your research. But by using AI you leverage this technology to market research faster.

Use the following our AI Market Research Prompts document.

The Prompts here include, but not limited to:

- Target Market
- Pains and Problems
- Objections
- Goals and Desires
- And Creation of Customer Avatar

### Compose

**Step 2: Compose your Initial Sales Video Script**

Now we have enough data from our AI Market Research, you can now start drafting our initial sales video script.

Use any of our proven sales video templates and use it as a prompt to let AI write your script for you.

### Customize

**Step 3: Customize to your Brand Voice**

Just like our previous example, use prompts to customize the result of AI to simplify it, translate to tagalog (if applicable) or manually edit it to fit your brand voice.

## Sales Funnel Copy

### Compile

**Step 1: Leverage your AI Market Research**

Since you already have done the market research, just build upon your thread and continue on it for your sales funnel copy.

### Compose

**Step 2: Compose your Sales Funnel Copy**

Use our proven sales page template as a reference in creating your sales funnel.

Use AI to create the elements needed in your sales page.

Your sales page must contain, but not limited to the following:

- Headline
- Sales Video
- Problem Section
- Benefits Section
- Unique Mechanism
- Guarantee *(if applicable)*
- FAQ Section

### Customize

**Step 3: Customize to Fit your Brand Voice**

As always, customize the results generated by AI to fit your brand voice.
