## Ebook Bonuses

One of the most effective ways to boost the perceived value of your product is by adding compelling bonuses. Digital information products, such as ebooks or guides, are perfect for this—they're inexpensive to produce and significantly enhance the value of your main offer.

When you increase the perceived value of your product with these high-value bonuses, you can justify charging a higher price, maximizing your revenue and giving your customers more reasons to buy.

**NOTE**: Your ebook bonuses should be related to your product. Your ebooks should help solve your target market's pains and problems or help them achieve desires.

Thus, you can continue in the same thread as your market research so that AI already has data for your audience and your product.
