# Sales Video Script

In this sales video script, we're gonna be using the script called PAS.

This means, Problem → Agitate → Solution.

We'll be identifying the problem that your dream customers have, agitate it so that it increases desire, and offer the solution which is our product or service.

I love this formula because people buy because they have a problem and they're willing to pay so that it gets solved and that's what the script will do.

We're telling them that our product is the solution to their problem.

Before sharing the fill-in-the-blanks script. Please answer the following questions below. After answering the questions, it will be easier for you to craft a converting sales video script.

What makes a sales video convert is not the high production value but the messaging. It's all about delivering the right message to the right audience. These questions will help you extract the important information so that your dream customer will buy.

- Who is your target audience?
- What results or outcome do they want to have?
- What are the biggest challenges or problems why your target audience is not achieving their dream outcome?
- What is the consequence if their challenges and problems will not be solved as soon as possible?
- What's your backstory? How did you discover the solution?
- What do you offer? Why is this the solution to their problem?
- What makes your product different from the rest? Why should I buy from you?
- Why should I buy now?

Take a blank sheet of paper and write down your answers.

Once you've accomplished this, then the rest would be easy since all you have to do is mention your answers in a presentation format.

## Here's the flow of the script

- Ask a question - Problems of your target audience
- Mention dream outcome of your audience
- Introduce yourself and share your backstory
- Mention the problems that your target audience
- Mention the consequence if the problem doesn't get solved
- Mention the solution to the problem
- Introduce your product
- Explain what problems it solves
- Explain what makes it different and why you're unique
- Reveal the regular price
- Justify the price
- Reveal the special price
- Discuss why they should buy now
- Explain how to buy
