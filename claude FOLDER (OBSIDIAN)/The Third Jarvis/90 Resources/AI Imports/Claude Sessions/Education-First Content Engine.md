# Education-First Content Engine (PH LaunchPoint)

Core idea: **you earn the right to sell by teaching first.** Trust before transaction — the ₱2,499 Franchise Market System becomes the obvious next step for someone you've already helped.

## Calendar balance

~80% value / 20% selling: 13 teaching posts (5 Franchise 101 + 8 Education) + 2 Pain + 2 Proof vs only 4 direct Offer posts.

- **Franchise 101 weekly series** every Monday (Ano ang franchising? / Magkano ang gastos? / Anong dokumento?)
- Two more education posts midweek, one pain/proof post, one offer post per week

## Soft-CTA rule

Teaching posts end with "save this / follow for more" only; hard CTAs reserved for Offer posts.
