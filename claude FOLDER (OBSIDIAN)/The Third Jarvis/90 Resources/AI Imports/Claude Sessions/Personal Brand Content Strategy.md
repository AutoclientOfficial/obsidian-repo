# Personal Brand Content Strategy

## The "Anolin Arc" (your winning post formula)

Story first → lesson second → franchising third. Your top posts make people *feel*, but historically never said what you do or gave a next step — that's why you were stuck at 1.1K followers with no partner leads.

## Strategy

- 4 content pillars, reels-first format mix (post to FB, TikTok, IG, Shorts)
- Hook bank + CTA ladder: every post ends with a real CTA (keyword comment → free guide → lead)
- Profile fixed to state what you do
- 90-day roadmap toward 50K; realistic timeline for 50K is 9–18 months, but **leads can start in weeks** once CTA keyword + free guide are live
- 5 starter captions written in Taglish voice, covering all pillars (incl. one for brand owners = supply side, one for buyers)
