# Growth Partner Pitch Deck Structure

6-slide close sequence (Canva-importable):

1. **This Partnership IS For You If…** — 6 qualifiers, gold checks
2. **This Is NOT For You If…** — 6 disqualifiers, red X (the takeaway)
3. Choose Your Growth Path (tiers)
4. Why the Growth System Is the Easy Yes
5. Our Guarantee
6. Let's Build Your Growth System (CTA)

## Why it works

Qualifier slides come **before** the offer to pre-frame the buyer before they see price. The "NOT for you" slide creates takeaway — prospects feel they must qualify themselves, which makes them want in.

## Delivery tip

Read the "NOT for you" slide slowly and confidently — that's the moment they feel you won't chase them. Then go straight to tiers.

## Open item

- Optional objection-handling/FAQ slide matched to the tiers
