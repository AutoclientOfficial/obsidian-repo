# How to Build a Funnel

1. Define the two paths: qualified → call; not qualified → product ([[Funnel Fundamentals]])
2. Build assessment/quiz with qualification logic ([[FBPH Funnel and Lead Qualification System]])
3. Landing page at ≤ grade-4 reading level, every CTA working ([[Landing Page Copy Rules]])
4. Wire backend: Apps Script/Sheets logging — new-version deployment, access=Anyone, no keepalive on big uploads ([[Quotation Form → Sheets → Payment Flow]])
5. Messenger flow: straight-up framing, no loan-provider vibes
6. Test end-to-end with fake lead before spending
7. Launch ads problem-first ([[Facebook Ads Essentials]]); review CPL weekly
