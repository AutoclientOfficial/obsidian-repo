# How to Close a Client

1. Lead completes Free Assessment (no assessment → no call)
2. Pre-call: review answers, check BANT ([[BANT Qualification]])
3. Discovery: run [[Discovery Questions]] + SPIN implication questions — get them to state the system gap
4. Present: qualifier slides first, "NOT for you" takeaway, then tiers ([[Growth Partner Pitch Deck Structure]])
5. Price with silence; handle objections via Acknowledge→Isolate→Reframe ([[Objection Handling]])
6. Close commitment with reservation/onboarding payment same-day
7. Contract: all fees upfront incl. recurring ([[FBPH Contracts Compliance and Risk Controls]])
8. Handoff to [[Client Delivery System]]
