## Content Strategy: FBPH Personal Brand

**Goal:** Not followers — franchise partner leads. Leads can start in weeks once the CTA ladder is live.

---

### The Stack (from your vault)

**Formula:** [Personal Brand Content Strategy]
`Story → Lesson → Franchising → CTA` (Anolin Arc)

**Ratio:** [Education-First Content Engine]
`80% value / 20% selling` — 4 teach posts for every 1 offer post

**Voice:** Taglish, short sentences, one idea per post

---

### 4 Content Pillars

| Pillar | Type | CTA |
|---|---|---|
| Franchise 101 | Education (weekly Mon) | Soft — "save this" |
| My Story / Wins | Story + proof | Soft → comment keyword |
| Pain Points | Problem-aware | Hard — DM / guide |
| Offer | Direct sell | Hard — book a call |

---

### Weekly Cadence (5 posts/week)

```
Mon — Franchise 101 (series)
Tue — Story / personal win
Wed — Education / myth bust
Thu — Pain point post
Fri — Proof or Offer
```

Format: Reels-first → repost to FB, TikTok, IG, Shorts.

---

### The Funnel (CTA Ladder)

```
Post → Keyword comment ("GUIDE")
         ↓
     Free Franchise Starter Guide  ← NOT BUILT YET
         ↓
     Warm lead DM sequence
         ↓
     Book a discovery call → partner deal
```

**Bottleneck right now: the lead magnet.** Nothing else in the funnel works without it.

---

### Build This First (in order)

1. **Franchise Starter Guide** — 1-page PDF or Canva doc. What is franchising, how much does it cost, how to pick one. This unlocks the whole CTA ladder.
2. **Keyword comment automation** — ManyChat or FB auto-reply to deliver the guide.
3. **10-post backlog** — 2 per pillar, scripted in Anolin Arc format, ready to publish.
4. **Bio fix** — state clearly what you do and who you help.
