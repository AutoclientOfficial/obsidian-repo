# Money Principles

1. **Price the outcome, not the hours.** A guaranteed pipeline is worth more than a service list.
2. **Commission + retainer beats either alone.** Setup fee funds the build; markup pays for the result; retainer keeps the machine running.
3. **Never let recurring fees be a surprise.** Agree on them upfront — renegotiating later costs goodwill (learned from the ₱30K → ₱15K concessions, see [[FBPH Pricing and Revenue Model]]).
4. **Cash collection is part of the process**, not an afterthought — collect onboarding payment at signing.
5. **Buffer every capital estimate.** Tight budgets (e.g. ₱6M branch builds) become your problem later.
