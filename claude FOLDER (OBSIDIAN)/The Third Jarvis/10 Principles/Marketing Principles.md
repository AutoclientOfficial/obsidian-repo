# Marketing Principles

1. **Education-first: ~80% teach / 20% sell.** You earn the right to sell by teaching ([[Education-First Content Engine]]).
2. **Problem-first ads, business-owner POV.** No hype words: never "guaranteed ROI," "passive income," "invest now" ([[FBPH Marketing Strategy and Creative Direction]]).
3. **Write at 4th-grade reading level.** Short words, ~10-word sentences ([[Landing Page Copy Rules]]).
4. **Every post ends with a real CTA**; soft CTAs on teaching posts, hard CTAs only on offer posts.
5. **Story → lesson → offer** (the "Anolin Arc"). Make them feel, then tell them what to do.
6. **Taglish voice, real Filipino business settings.** Make owners feel seen, not sold to.
