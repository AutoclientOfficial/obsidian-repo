# Business Principles

1. **Sell growth partnership with a guaranteed pipeline — never "marketing services."** The frame changes the price ceiling.
2. **Productize, don't customize.** One default package; customization is the exception, priced accordingly.
3. **Capacity rule: 3–5 deep-service clients max.** Beyond 5, hire an Account Manager or burnout is guaranteed.
4. **Ad spend is always separate and client-funded.** Never subsidize.
5. **Recurring revenue is the goal state.** Every one-time deal should create a retainer path.
6. **FBPH never holds investor funds and never guarantees returns.** Compliance is a moat, not a burden. See [[FBPH Contracts Compliance and Risk Controls]].
7. **Focus beats portfolio.** 2026 priorities: FBPH, AI Agency, Marketing — everything else paused ([[Business Priorities 2026]]).
