# AI Principles

1. **AI is the fuel; the business is the engine.** Sell outcomes, integrate AI as the advantage inside every engagement ([[AI Agency Relationship]]).
2. **The vault is the single source of truth.** Every AI (Claude, ChatGPT, plugins) reads from and writes back to Obsidian.
3. **Automate the repeatable, keep humans on judgment.** Qualification, follow-up, reporting → AI. Closing, strategy, relationships → humans.
4. **Imported AI knowledge starts as `theory`.** Validate before it becomes policy ([[Epistemic Status Convention]]).
5. **Every client system should include an AI layer** — front desk, qualification, nurturing, booking ([[FBPH Service Stack]]).
