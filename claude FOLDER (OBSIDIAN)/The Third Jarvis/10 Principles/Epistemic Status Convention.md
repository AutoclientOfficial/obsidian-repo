# Epistemic Status Convention

Every knowledge/principle note carries a `status` field in frontmatter. Separate what you know from what you believe from what you've proven.

| Status | Meaning | Use when |
|---|---|---|
| `theory` | Learned from books/courses/AI, not yet tested by us | New frameworks, imported knowledge |
| `hypothesis` | Currently being tested in a real campaign/deal | Active experiments |
| `validated` | Proven through our own results | Closed deals, measured campaigns |

Rules: default to `theory` for anything imported. Upgrade only with evidence (name the client/campaign in the note). Review `hypothesis` notes monthly. This matters most in AI and marketing, where popular ideas often fail in real execution.

Exception: reference notes carry **no** status — MOCs, indexes, templates, dashboards, archive notes. Status applies to claims, not containers. FBPH strategy notes currently running in the market are `hypothesis` until a campaign or closed deal proves them.
