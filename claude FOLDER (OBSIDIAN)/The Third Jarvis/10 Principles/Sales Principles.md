# Sales Principles

1. **Sell transformation, never documents.** They buy growth and scale, not decks and CRMs ([[FBPH Sales Philosophy]]).
2. **Qualify hard, early.** Assessment before call; unqualified leads get the education product, not your time ([[FBPH Funnel and Lead Qualification System]]).
3. **Takeaway selling works.** "This is NOT for you if…" makes buyers qualify themselves ([[Growth Partner Pitch Deck Structure]]).
4. **Diagnose before prescribing.** Discovery reveals the system gap; the offer fills it.
5. **Pre-frame before price.** Qualifiers come before the offer slide, always.
6. **Never overpromise.** No guaranteed sales, ROI, or approvals — ever.
