# FBPH Sales Scripts and Messaging Library

## Preferred Tone

Daniel's preferred FBPH tone:

- Modern Taglish
- Short
- Casual but professional
- Direct
- Not AI-sounding
- Excited but calm
- Value-based
- Business-owner focused

## Common CTA

Primary CTA:

- Book a meeting
- Answer the Free Assessment
- Check if franchise-ready
- Apply for FBPH / Launch Point offer

## General Expansion Inquiry Message

Used for clients interested in expansion without spending capital:

> May nakita kaming potential sa business mo for expansion.
>
> Instead na ikaw lahat ang gumastos sa new branch, pwede natin siyang i-structure para partner-funded or franchise-ready.
>
> Gumawa kami ng assessment para makita kung ready na ba business mo for expansion or may kailangan munang ayusin sa system.
>
> Sagutan mo lang ito, then if qualified, we can schedule a quick call.

## Message to Business Owners Who Commented on Capital Post

Direction requested:

- Straight up
- Tell them FBPH created an assessment
- Assess whether they are franchise-ready or not
- Lead to Free Assessment

Core message structure:

```text
Hi [Name], nakita ko comment mo.
Gumawa kami ng quick assessment for business owners para malaman kung ready na ba yung business for franchising, partnership, or expansion funding.
Sagutan mo muna ito para makita natin kung qualified ka for our DFY partnership with FBPH o kung mas okay muna na i-prepare yung system mo.
Link: https://www.franchisebrokerageph.online/free-assessment-page
```

## Supplier / Brand Launch Message

Used when scheduling with a supplier and preparing scripts/storyboards:

Key points:

- Schedule with supplier
- Create scripts and storyboard for 4 brands
- Estimated at least 2 days
- Planned launch: **July 15**
- Hope to close by end of the month

## Client Payment Message Style

For client Gem / SQOE payment message:

- QR code for payment of **₱10,000**
- Purpose: system and partnership
- Tone: excited, casual, Taglish

## Welcome Kit Message Style

For Sir Gem / SQOE:

- PDF is the welcome kit
- Short message
- Tone: exciting, calm, Modern Taglish

## Sales Conversation Principles

FBPH sales should focus on:

- Diagnosing readiness
- Clarifying expansion goals
- Avoiding overpromising
- Showing the system gap
- Moving qualified leads to call
- Redirecting unqualified leads to digital product / preparation resource
