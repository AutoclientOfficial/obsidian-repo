# FBPH Pricing and Revenue Model

## Standard FBPH Revenue Model

FBPH pricing and revenue models discussed across conversations include several versions.

Current and recurring concepts:

| Revenue Item | Amount / Rate | Notes |
|---|---:|---|
| Setup Fee | ₱50,000–₱100,000 | Used for media day, deck, landing page, CRM, sales team setup |
| Common Setup Fee | ₱100,000 | Frequently used as full setup fee |
| Brokerage Commission | 25% markup | Added on top of client's base offer / total capital |
| Sales Agent Commission | 5% of FBPH commission | Used in Eagles examples |
| Sales Agent Commission Option | 3% of FBPH commission | Later alternative option |

## 25% Markup Model

FBPH's main brokerage model has been:

```text
Client Base Capital + 25% FBPH Markup = Public / Partner Offer
```

Example discussed:

| Item | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Total partner deposit | ₱5,000,000 |
| FBPH commission included | ₱1,000,000 |
| Sales commission at 5% of FBPH commission | ₱50,000 |

## Setup Fee Inclusions

The setup fee has been described as covering:

- Media day
- Franchise / investor deck
- Landing page
- CRM
- Sales system
- Scripts
- Sales team setup
- Basic content and campaign structure

## Alternative Commission Models Discussed

### 20% Net Sales / Partnership Model

A general contract version discussed:

- FBPH earns **20% commission on net sales**
- OPEX is deducted first
- Taxes are paid by the client
- FBPH receives a clean net amount
- Dividends / payouts every **15th and 30th**
- Term: **5 years**

⚠️ This appears to be an alternative or older model, not always the standard franchise brokerage model.

### 100k + 15% vs 25% Markup

Another pricing direction discussed:

| Model | Structure |
|---|---|
| Option 1 | ₱100,000 recurring + 15% commission |
| Option 2 | 25% markup |

⚠️ Needs current confirmation before using in contracts.

## Recurring Fee Negotiation History

For some clients, a ₱30,000 monthly recurring fee was challenged.

Negotiation path discussed:

1. Original monthly recurring: **₱30,000**
2. Client objected because no prior agreement
3. Proposed concession: **₱15,000/month**
4. Later target: **₱10,000/month recurring**

Use ⚠️ when applying this because the final rate may differ by client.
