# Archived and Experimental FBPH Models

## Purpose

This note captures older, experimental, or uncertain FBPH business models from past conversations.

Use these only with caution.

## Trucking / Financing Model

Older FBPH trucking model:

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Borrower downpayment | 30% |
| Lender funding | 70% |
| Lender interest | 1.2%–1.3% monthly |
| FBPH income | Processing fee of 5%–8% |
| Additional FBPH income | Share of interest / first checks |
| Partner source | Romel knows trucking owners and helps source buyers |
| Default protection | Repossession or continued payment via casa/truck seller |
| Documents | Promissory note and PDCs |

⚠️ This may not be part of the current FBPH franchise brokerage direction.
