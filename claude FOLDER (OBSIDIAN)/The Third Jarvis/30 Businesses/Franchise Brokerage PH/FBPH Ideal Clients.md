# FBPH Ideal Clients

## Primary Market

Philippine SMEs.

Businesses already operating successfully that want expansion.

## Typical Characteristics

- Proven business
- Existing revenue
- Wants multiple branches
- Doesn't know how to franchise
- Doesn't have expansion systems
- Doesn't have investor acquisition

## Expansion Methods

- Franchise
- Partnership
- Investor-funded branches

## Pain Points

- Owner doing everything
- No sales team
- No marketing team
- No expansion documents
- No CRM
- No investor network

## Desired Result

Scale without hiring an entire internal expansion department.
