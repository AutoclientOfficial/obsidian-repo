# FBPH Marketing Strategy and Creative Direction

## Core Philosophy

Educate before selling. Become the trusted authority on business expansion.

Position FBPH as **"the team behind business expansion"** — not "the company selling franchise packages."

Long-term objective: inbound demand — business owners seek FBPH because of expertise, not outbound prospecting.

## Main Content Pillars

Franchising · Business Growth · Expansion Strategy · Investor Education · Success Stories · AI for Business · Systems · Case Studies

## Funnel

```text
Content
→ Lead Magnet
→ Landing Page
→ Appointment
→ Discovery
→ Expansion Plan
→ Proposal
→ Client
```

## Main Marketing Shift

The major creative direction shift for FBPH / Launch Point PH:

### Before

Ads looked like:

- Ebook announcement
- Product promo
- Template bundle
- Generic franchise offer

### Now

Ads should feel like a real business owner problem:

> "Ready ka na mag-franchise… pero ready ba ang system mo?"

## What FBPH Is Really Selling

FBPH is not only selling an ebook or service.

It is selling:

- Franchise readiness
- Expansion preparation
- A system before talking to serious partners
- Confidence to present the business properly
- Investor / franchise partner attraction

## Core Message

> Hindi sapat na may sales. Kung hindi ready ang backend system mo, mahirap maka-attract ng serious franchise partners or investors.

## Target Market

Filipino business owners who:

- Already have a running business
- Want to franchise or expand
- May have interested partners or investors
- Feel they need guidance
- Do not want to spend **₱1,000,000** on consultants
- Are already open for franchising
- Are planning to franchise soon

## Owner Pain Statements

Use these instead of direct investment hype:

- "Sayang demand."
- "Hindi ko na kaya branches."
- "May gustong mag-invest pero wala akong system."
- "Lumalaki pero ako lahat gumagawa."
- "May nagtatanong kung open for franchise, pero hindi ko alam paano i-present."
- "May potential ang business, pero hindi pa ready ang backend."
- "Hindi ka kulang sa potential. Kulang ka lang sa system."

## Avoided Ad Language

Avoid:

- "Invest now"
- "Franchise opportunity"
- "Looking for investors"
- "Guaranteed ROI"
- "Passive income"
- "Earn monthly"

## Image Ad Direction

Preferred style:

- Problem-first
- Business-owner POV
- Taglish narrative
- Emotional but not fearmongering
- Real Filipino business setting
- Show, don't tell
- No heavy ebook focus
- No large book mockup
- No cluttered CTA inside image
- Avoid overexplaining with subheadline
- Use situations that make owners feel seen

## Example Visual Directions Discussed

### Franchise Readiness Problem

Business owner in shop / restaurant looking at papers, laptop, or inquiries.

Message implied:

- They have demand
- They have interested partners
- But no system yet

### Investor Proposal Skepticism

Photorealistic overhead shot:

- Wooden café table
- Formal proposal document
- Highlighted peso amount
- Black coffee
- Cheap ballpen
- Filipino male hand pointing skeptically
- Warm natural light
- No text in image

### Storefront Risk Metaphor

Photorealistic storefront:

- Proud, well-lit small Filipino restaurant at dusk
- Owner silhouette arranging chairs
- Wet pavement reflection looks darker and distorted
- Protective emotional tone
- No text in image
