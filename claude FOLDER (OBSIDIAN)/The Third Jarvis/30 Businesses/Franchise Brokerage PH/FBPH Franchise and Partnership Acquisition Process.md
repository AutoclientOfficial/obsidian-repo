# FBPH Franchise and Partnership Acquisition Process

## End-to-End FBPH Process

The FBPH process was described as running from ads up to grand opening.

High-level flow:

1. Client onboarding
2. Business diagnosis
3. Offer structuring
4. Franchise / partner positioning
5. Asset creation
6. Funnel setup
7. Ads and lead generation
8. Lead qualification
9. Sales call / partner presentation
10. Document preparation
11. Payment / confirmation
12. Partner turnover
13. Branch setup support
14. Soft opening
15. Grand opening

## Phase 1 — Client Onboarding

Core activities:

- Confirm business goals
- Clarify expansion model
- Identify ideal franchisee / investor / partner
- Collect brand information
- Define offer structure
- Clarify whether the model is franchise, partnership, co-ownership, or licensing

## Phase 2 — Expansion Asset Build

Assets discussed:

- Franchise / partner deck
- Offer structure
- Investor presentation
- Landing page
- CRM
- Application form
- Auto-reply scripts
- Sales scripts
- Follow-up flow
- Branch readiness checklist
- Partner qualification process

## Phase 3 — Funnel and Lead Generation

Typical funnel components:

- Meta ads
- Landing page
- Lead capture form
- Messenger / WhatsApp automation
- Botcake-style message flows
- CRM / inquiry tracking sheet
- Appointment booking
- Sales call
- Partner application / assessment

## Phase 4 — Sales and Screening

Lead screening should check:

- Available capital
- Business background
- Location interest
- Readiness timeline
- Decision-maker status
- Fit with brand values
- Understanding of risk
- Willingness to attend meeting / call

## Phase 5 — Closing and Documentation

Documents discussed across projects:

- Letter of Intent
- Partner Application Form
- Shareholders Agreement
- Brokerage Acknowledgment
- Non-Circumvention Agreement
- Service Completion and Turnover Confirmation
- Payment Receipt
- Franchise / partnership agreement
- Branch-specific investor packet

## Phase 6 — Turnover

FBPH's role after closing:

- Confirm introduction is completed
- Turn over investor / franchisee to the brand
- Confirm documentation flow
- Confirm that operations are handled by franchisor / brand owner
- Avoid managing client funds
- Avoid making performance guarantees

## Soft Opening Guidelines Example

For a soft opening / groundbreaking event, discussed structure included:

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Call time at venue | 7:30 AM |
| Departure from Rockwell | 6:30 AM |
| Run-through meeting | 8:00 AM |
| Event start | 11:00 AM |
| Construction progress | 75%–80% complete |
| Mood | Everyone should be excited to meet partners |
| Message | Construction has been ongoing for around 1 month |
| Goal | Show partners what has already happened |
| Grand opening target | 27th ⚠️ month not captured |
