# FBPH Funnel and Lead Qualification System

## Core Funnel

FBPH funnel direction:

```text
Ad / Organic Post
→ Comment or Click
→ Messenger / Botcake Flow
→ Free Assessment
→ Qualification Result
→ Book a Call OR Redirect to Ebook Page
```

## Free Assessment Funnel

The Free Assessment was built for business owners who want to know whether they are franchise-ready.

Assessment link used:

```text
https://www.franchisebrokerageph.online/free-assessment-page
```

## Two Possible Assessment Outcomes

| Outcome | Destination | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Qualified | Book a call with FBPH | Potential DFY partnership / Launch Point offer |
| Not qualified | Redirect to ebook page | Sell educational product / Franchise Marketing System |

## Botcake Message Direction

A message flow was requested for people commenting on a business capital post.

Updated positioning:

- Be straight up.
- Tell them FBPH created something to assess if they are franchise-ready or not.
- Avoid sounding like a generic loan / capital provider.
- Lead them to the assessment.

## Business Capital Lead Criteria

Original capital post targeted:

- Existing business owners
- Looking for capital
- Minimum capital need: **₱5M**
- Up to **₱100M**
- Non-collateral business offer
- Requirements:
  - 2 valid IDs
  - Business permit such as DTI / BIR / Mayor's Permit
  - Physical store
  - More than 2 years of operation

## Qualification Logic

Key qualification factors discussed:

- Existing business
- Physical store
- More than 2 years operating
- Has permits
- Has potential for expansion
- Needs capital or partner-funded growth
- Wants franchising or investor-backed expansion
- Willing to go through assessment / call

## Lead Handling Principle

FBPH wants to avoid wasting time on unqualified leads.

Important filter language:

- "Para masala ko lang yung mga totoo at quality business."
- "We created something that will assess if franchise-ready ka na or not yet."
- "Kung qualified ka, you can book a call with us."
- "Kung not yet, may resource kami para ma-prepare mo muna business mo."
