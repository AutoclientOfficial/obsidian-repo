# FBPH Business Overview

## Business Name

Franchise Brokerage PH (FBPH)

## Core Mission

Help business owners expand through franchising, partnerships, and investor-funded branches without requiring them to build their own internal expansion team.

## Positioning

FBPH is not simply a franchise consultant.

It acts as the outsourced Expansion Team for business owners.

Instead of hiring:

- Marketing Team
- Sales Team
- Investor Acquisition Team
- Appointment Setters
- Closers
- CRM Specialists
- Content Creators

FBPH provides the complete expansion engine.

## Core Value Proposition

A business owner should only focus on:

- Operating the business
- Maintaining product quality
- Supporting franchisees

FBPH handles the growth engine.

## Problems FBPH Solves

- No franchise sales team
- No investor acquisition system
- No marketing team
- No appointment setting
- No CRM
- No follow-up system
- No franchise presentation
- No expansion strategy
- No content marketing
- No sales funnel

## Long-Term Vision

Become the preferred expansion partner for Philippine SMEs looking to scale through:

- Franchising
- Partnerships
- Investors
- Multi-branch expansion
