- Independent contractor
- Marketing and brokerage service provider
- Lead generation provider
- Screening and referral partner
- Facilitator of introductions and documentation flow

FBPH should not be documented as:

- Investment solicitor
- Fundraiser
- Securities broker
- Financing intermediary
- Holder of investor money
- Guarantor of business performance

## Important Clauses to Include

### Limitation of Authority

Section reference used:

```text
Section 1.5 — Limitation of Authority
```

Meaning:

- FBPH cannot bind the client unless authorized.
- FBPH cannot make promises outside written materials.
- FBPH cannot guarantee investor approval, sales, ROI, or branch performance.

### Accuracy of Representations

Section reference used:

```text
Section 3.5 — Accuracy of Representations
```

Meaning:

- Client must provide truthful and accurate financial, operational, and business information.
- FBPH relies on client-provided data for marketing and presentations.

### Prohibition on Receipt / Holding of Funds

Section reference used:

```text
Section 3.6 — Prohibition on Receipt/Holding of Funds
```

Meaning:

- FBPH should not receive or hold investor / partner funds.
- Payments should go directly to the client / franchisor / project company unless a separate written structure exists.
