## 1. BUSINESS MODEL & POSITIONING

### Core Model

- FBPH is a **franchise brokerage and growth partnership** business — not a marketing agency.
- Revenue structure: **₱100K onboarding + 25% markup** on closed deals (35% total commission structure with equity shares and sales agent commissions in the tracker).
- Positioning rule: sell **growth partnership with a guaranteed pipeline**, never "marketing services." The frame changes the price ceiling.

### Retainer Tiers (Growth Partner Model)

|Tier|Scope|Price|
|---|---|---|
|Tier 1 — Single Brand Brokerage Partner|Standard deliverables|₱60K–₱80K/month + 25% markup on closed deals|
|Tier 2 — Premium Growth Partner|+ QBR, investor matching priority|₱100K–₱150K/month + 25% markup|
|Ad spend|Always separate, client-funded|Never subsidize|

### Capacity Rules

- Current setup: **3–5 deep-service clients max.** Beyond 5, hire an Account Manager or burnout is guaranteed.
- **Productize, don't customize.** Lock one default package. Per-client customization eats margin and time.

### Two-Engine Framework

- **Engine 1 — Brand Onboarding:** acquire and onboard franchise brands (SOP v1.1, Welcome Kit, pitch deck).
- **Engine 2 — Investor Pipeline:** match investors (especially OFW segment) to onboarded brands.
- Company docs organized by **department architecture**: Marketing, Sales, Operations, HR, Finance — replicated per venture from the FBPH template.

## 2. FUNNEL ARCHITECTURE — Two-Path Conversion

### Entry Point

**Free Assessment** posted via Facebook Groups → segments leads by readiness/capital.

### Path A — DFY (Done-For-You)

Qualified leads → main landing page → schedule a meeting → **DFY franchise partnership** (high-touch, premium).

### Path B — DIY (Do-It-Yourself)

Other leads → second landing page → **DIY ebook product** at a deep discount from original value (self-serve, volume play).

### Why It Works

- Nobody leaves empty-handed: high-capital leads get the partnership, low-capital leads still convert on a product.
- The Free Assessment doubles as a qualification filter AND a value-first lead magnet.
- DIY buyers become a nurture pool for future DFY upgrades.

## 3. SALES SYSTEM

### The 6-Stage Franchise Sales Funnel

Inquiry → Qualification → Discovery → Presentation → Closing → Onboarding

### Conversion Math (per 100 leads)

100 inquiries → 40 qualified → 20 discovery calls → 10 presentations → 5 serious → **2–3 closed deals**

> Selling is a system, not a talent. Track the ratios, fix the weakest stage.

### Lead Generation — 5 Core Channels

1. **Franchise expos** (PFA, AFFI) — ₱15K–₱50K per event, 20–100+ qualified inquiries each
2. **Facebook lead ads**
3. **OFW network** (communities, forums, remittance-adjacent audiences)
4. **Referrals**
5. **Franchise directories**

### Objection Handling (Bilingual Format)

Tagalog objection as the heading, structured English/Taglish response. The 7 classics include:

- _"Ang mahal naman."_ → reframe to ROI and payback period, not price
- Family barrier objections → involve the decision-making circle early in the closing sequence
- All objections answered with system + proof, never discount-first

### Closing System

- **7-step closing sequence**, includes handling the family barrier
- **3-payment structure table** as a closing tool
- AMLA compliance warning on large cash payments
- CRM discipline: **8 lead fields per record, Friday pipeline review ritual, cold-lead reactivation** cadence

## 5. OFFER BUILDING & PITCH FRAMEWORK

### Value Stack Rules (from slide 92 work)

1. **The math must add up.** If line items say ₱138,995, don't claim ₱149,995+. Sharp clients will catch it — instant credibility loss. Fix the total or genuinely add items.
2. **Reframe deliverables as outcomes.** Not "CRM Automation System" — instead, what the client gets back (time, booked calls, revenue).
3. **Visible price reveal** — the stacked value vs. actual price gap is the persuasion engine. Show both numbers.
4. **Risk reversal** — results-or-we-keep-working-free guarantee. Removes the last objection.
5. **Closer slide centers on client transformation**, not agency capability. Three tested angles:
    - Freedom angle
    - Pain-to-relief angle
    - **Partnership/identity angle** ← strongest for high-ticket

## 6. LANDING PAGE CONVERSION PLAYBOOK (SQOE learnings)

- **4th-grade reading level.** Simplify all copy. Short sentences win.
- **Scarcity mechanism must be structural, not fake:** "One partner per city. When your city is taken, it is taken." Scarcity built into the business model itself.
- **Pattern breaker:** one section in a contrasting color (black in an orange/white page) reserved for the scarcity/urgency message — it dominates because it's the only one.
- **Alternating section rhythm** (white/orange/white...) keeps scroll momentum; button colors alternate for contrast.
- **Frictionless CTA framing:** "Applying is free. The only thing you can lose by waiting is your city." → CTA: "Check If My City Is Still Open" (curiosity + urgency in one button).
- **Social proof widget:** cycling verified-client popup — but **never launch with fabricated names.** Replace placeholders with real applicants before going live (platform rules + integrity).
- **Mobile-first:** most ad traffic is mobile; every layout must stack cleanly.
- **GHL deployment:** strip document tags, scope CSS under a wrapper ID, prefix keyframes, use 100vw breakout for full-width sections.

## 7. CLIENT RETENTION — Monthly Recurring Service Model

### Deliverables (Growth Partner Package)

- **Marketing:** 2 ad campaigns/month (bi-weekly refresh), 1 video shoot → 4–6 creatives + 2–3 reels, 12 organic posts, 1 broadcast to lead database
- **Pipeline:** 15–20 qualified investor leads/month, 8–12 discovery calls, weekly warm-lead follow-up, 1 OFW-targeted push/month
- **Reporting:** 2 performance reports/month, 1 monthly strategy deck, 1 competitor/market scan
- **Brand:** 2 new testimonials/month target, review monitoring, 1 PR/feature pitch/month

### Meeting Cadence (final — premium 2-meeting model)

|When|Meeting|Duration|
|---|---|---|
|Week 2|Mid-Month Performance & Creative Sync|60 min|
|Week 4|Monthly Business Review + Next Month Strategy|90 min|
|Quarterly|QBR — deep dive (financials, market position, roadmap)|2–3 hrs|
|Fridays|Async update via Viber/Messenger|—|

**Retention principle:** Don't over-meet. Visible, structured deliverables — not frequent check-ins — are the marker of premium service. The QBR is the make-or-break for retention.

## 8. STRATEGIC OPERATING PRINCIPLES

1. **Systems over talent.** Every function (sales, marketing, ops) gets a documented, trackable system with checkboxes and ratios.
2. **One canonical document per function.** Duplicates and copy-paste drift kill execution (CEO Command Center lesson: 124 docs, 11 duplicate/conflict issues found).
3. **Integrity is non-negotiable:** no fabricated social proof, no falsified reports, transparent ad-spend reconciliation, math that adds up in every offer.
4. **Premium = fewer, better touchpoints** backed by visible output.
5. **Speak to one persona at a time.** Segmented messaging beats broad messaging every time.
6. **Track the funnel math weekly.** 100 → 40 → 20 → 10 → 5 → 2–3. The number that drops tells you where to work.
7. **Productize the offer, protect the margin, respect capacity limits.**
