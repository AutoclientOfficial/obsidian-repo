# FBPH Company Core and Positioning

## Company

**Franchise Brokerage PH (FBPH)** is Daniel Liwanag's franchise brokerage and expansion company in the Philippines.

FBPH has been positioned as an **external expansion team for brands** that want to grow through:

- Franchising
- Partnership-funded branches
- Investor-funded expansion
- Strategic co-franchise / co-ownership structures
- Franchisee / partner matching

## Core Positioning

FBPH helps business owners who already have demand, customers, or expansion interest but lack the system to attract serious partners.

Common positioning lines used:

- "We help business owners build expansion and investor-attraction systems."
- "FBPH is your external expansion team."
- "Hindi sapat na may sales. Kailangan ready ang backend system."
- "May business kang pwedeng i-expand. Kulang ka lang sa system."
- "Ready ka na mag-franchise… pero ready ba ang system mo?"

## What FBPH Actually Solves

FBPH solves the gap between a business owner's expansion potential and their lack of infrastructure.

Specific gaps discussed:

- No investor / franchise deck
- No offer structure
- No sales scripts
- No CRM
- No follow-up system
- No landing page
- No appointment setters
- No closers
- No documentation
- No operations readiness
- No franchise / partnership positioning

## Services

FBPH service scope includes:

- Franchise offer structuring
- Investor / partner presentation
- Pitch deck creation
- Landing page creation
- CRM setup
- Lead generation
- Appointment setting
- Sales closers
- Content and ads direction
- Documentation support
- Investor acquisition system
- Partner screening
- Client onboarding flow

## Legal / Compliance Positioning

FBPH should be positioned as:

- Marketing service provider
- Lead generation provider
- Brokerage / facilitation partner
- Matching service
- Coordination and turnover partner

FBPH should **not** be positioned as:

- Investment solicitor
- Fundraiser
- Financing intermediary
- Securities broker
- Owner/operator of client branches
- Guarantor of returns

## Important Compliance Language

Use language like:

- "Brokerage and facilitation only"
- "FBPH does not hold investor funds"
- "FBPH does not guarantee sales, profit, ROI, or branch performance"
- "Applicants must conduct their own due diligence"
- "Final approval remains with the brand owner / franchisor"
