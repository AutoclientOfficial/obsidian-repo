# FBPH Sales Philosophy

## Primary Principle

Sell transformation.

Never sell documents.

## Instead of Saying

"We make franchise manuals."

## Say

"We build the systems that allow businesses to expand."

## Customer Buys

Not:

- Manual
- Deck
- CRM

They buy:

- Growth
- Expansion
- More branches
- More revenue
- Scale

## Internal Reminder

Always shift conversations toward business outcomes. Not deliverables.
