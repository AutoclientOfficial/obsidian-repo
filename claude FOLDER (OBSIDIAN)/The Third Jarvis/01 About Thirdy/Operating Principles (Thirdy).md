# Operating Principles (Thirdy)

1. **Build systems businesses cannot live without.** Don't compete with businesses.
2. **Truth over agreement.** Feedback must be honest, even when uncomfortable.
3. **Productize, don't customize.** Custom work eats margin and time.
4. **Recurring beats one-time.** Every offer should evolve toward a retainer.
5. **Teach first, sell second.** Authority is earned through education.
6. **If it happens twice, write the SOP.**
7. **Knowledge lives in the vault, not in chat threads.**
