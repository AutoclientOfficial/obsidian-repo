# Content Machine

Repeatable weekly content production for FBPH / personal brand.

**Cadence** (from [[Education-First Content Engine]]): Mon Franchise 101 · midweek 2 education posts · 1 pain/proof post · 1 offer post. ~80% teach / 20% sell.

**Pipeline**: idea bank (from client questions) → hook ([[Hooks & Content Frameworks]]) → draft in Taglish voice → CTA per post type → schedule → weekly metrics review.

**Inputs**: [[Personal Brand Content Strategy]] pillars. **Output metric**: CTA keyword comments → assessment starts.
