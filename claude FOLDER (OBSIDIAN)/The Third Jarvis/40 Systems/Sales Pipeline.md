# Sales Pipeline

Stages: Inquiry → Assessment done → Call booked → Call held → Proposal → Signed → Onboarding paid.

- Track conversion % between every stage; the worst ratio is the week's focus ([[Systems Thinking]])
- No call without BANT-B+N ([[BANT Qualification]])
- Proposal follows [[Growth Partner Pitch Deck Structure]]
- Payment collected at signing ([[Money Principles]])
- CRM hygiene is a daily SOP, not a mood
