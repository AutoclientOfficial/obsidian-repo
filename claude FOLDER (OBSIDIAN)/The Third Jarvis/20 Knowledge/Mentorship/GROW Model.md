# GROW Model

Session structure for coaching calls:

1. **Goal** — what do you want from this session/quarter?
2. **Reality** — what's actually happening? (numbers, not feelings)
3. **Options** — generate 3+ paths before judging any
4. **Will** — what exactly will you do, by when, and how will I know?

Works for client QBRs and internal 1-on-1s alike. Pairs with [[Coaching Question Frameworks]].
