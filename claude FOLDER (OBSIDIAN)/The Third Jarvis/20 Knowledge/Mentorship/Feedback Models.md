# Feedback Models

- **SBI**: Situation → Behavior → Impact ("In yesterday's client call (S), the pricing wasn't anchored first (B), so the client fixated on cost (I)")
- **Radical Candor**: care personally + challenge directly; ruinous empathy (avoiding hard truths) is the common failure — aligns with "truth over agreement"
- Feedback is a system: weekly cadence, scoreboard-based, no surprises (Sports Culture — [[FBPH Operations OS Team and Hiring]])
