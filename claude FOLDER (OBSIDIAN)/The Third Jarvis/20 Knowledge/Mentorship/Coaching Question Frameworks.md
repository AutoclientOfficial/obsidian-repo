# Coaching Question Frameworks

Coaching = asking, not telling. High-leverage questions:

- "What does winning look like in 90 days?"
- "What's the real constraint — knowledge, system, or courage?"
- "What would you do if you couldn't fail? What's the 10% version of that you can test this week?"
- "What are you tolerating that you shouldn't?"
- "If you were coaching someone in your exact situation, what would you tell them?"

Rule: silence after the question. The second answer is usually the true one.
