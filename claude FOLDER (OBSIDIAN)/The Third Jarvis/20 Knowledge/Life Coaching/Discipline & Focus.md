# Discipline & Focus

- **Focus is subtraction.** The 2026 decision to pause Via Arts / Unlock PH / Choco Ecom *is* the strategy ([[Business Priorities 2026]])
- One priority per day placed before reactive work
- Timeboxing beats to-do lists; the calendar is the plan
- Energy management > time management: sell/create in peak hours, admin in troughs
- Known weakness in the team: operations & consistency — countermeasure is systems and scoreboards, not motivation ([[Operating Principles (Thirdy)]])
