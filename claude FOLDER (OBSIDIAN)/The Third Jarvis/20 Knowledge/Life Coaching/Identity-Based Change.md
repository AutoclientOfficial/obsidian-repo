# Identity-Based Change

(James Clear) Lasting change works outside-in poorly and inside-out well: **decide who you are, then act like that person.**

- Outcome goals: "close 5 clients" → Identity: "I'm the operator who documents and delegates"
- Every action is a vote for an identity; small reps compound
- Application: "builder who ships systems" beats "hustler who grinds" — the first scales, the second burns out
- Reframe failure: a missed week is data about the system, not a verdict on the person
