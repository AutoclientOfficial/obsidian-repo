# Moats & Competitive Advantage

Durable advantages, roughly in order of strength for a service business:

1. **Switching costs** — when the client's CRM, funnel, scripts, and pipeline all run on your system, leaving is expensive (our real moat)
2. **Network effects** — investor pool × brand portfolio: each side makes the other more valuable (the FBPH two-engine flywheel)
3. **Brand/authority** — education-first content compounds into "the team behind expansion"
4. **Process power** — documented SOPs and playbooks that competitors can't copy quickly

Principle: "Don't compete with businesses. Build systems businesses cannot live without."
