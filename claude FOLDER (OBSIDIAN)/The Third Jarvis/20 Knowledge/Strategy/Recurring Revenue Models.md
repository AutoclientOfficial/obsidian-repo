# Recurring Revenue Models

Ladder from worst to best revenue quality:

1. One-time project (setup fees)
2. Commission on events (25% markup on closes — lumpy but aligned)
3. Retainer for service (₱30K–₱100K/mo agency tiers)
4. Retainer for infrastructure (AI/chatbot/website ₱10–15K/mo — survives service churn)
5. Equity/participation (20% models — highest upside, highest risk ⚠️)

Target blend per client: setup + commission + infrastructure retainer. Retention rule: the retainer should be tied to something running (AI, CRM, hosting), not just "hours."
