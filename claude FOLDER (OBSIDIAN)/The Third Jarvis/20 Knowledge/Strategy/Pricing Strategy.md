# Pricing Strategy

- **Price the frame, not the labor.** "Growth partnership" prices 3–5× above "marketing services" for identical work.
- **Three tiers, middle designed to win.** Decoy high tier raises the ceiling ([[FBPH Internal Marketing Agency Offers]]).
- **Anchor on the alternative**: in-house team payroll or consultant fees (₱1M), never on competitors' retainers.
- **Raise prices via new clients, not renegotiation.**
- **Concessions trade, never gift**: drop the fee only for scope cut, longer term, or case-study rights.
