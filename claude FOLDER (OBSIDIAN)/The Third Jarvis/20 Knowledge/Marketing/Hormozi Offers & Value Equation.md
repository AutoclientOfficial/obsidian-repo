# Hormozi Offers & Value Equation

**Value = (Dream Outcome × Perceived Likelihood) / (Time Delay × Effort & Sacrifice)**

Raise the top, shrink the bottom. A "Grand Slam Offer" is so good people feel stupid saying no.

Levers:

- **Dream outcome**: sell the expansion/pipeline, not the deliverables
- **Likelihood**: proof, case studies, guarantees (FBPH: guarantee the pipeline, never the ROI)
- **Time delay**: fast first wins (e.g., leads in weeks)
- **Effort**: done-for-you beats done-with-you at premium prices
- Stack: bonuses, scarcity ("One Partner Per City" — see [[SQOE Guitars Franchise System]]), urgency, naming
