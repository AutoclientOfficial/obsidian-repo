# Hooks & Content Frameworks

A hook's only job: stop the scroll and earn the next 3 seconds.

Hook patterns that work in PH business content:

- Contrarian: "Myth: pang-mayaman lang ang franchising. Mali."
- Number + stake: "₱425K ang isang 2.5% share. Alam mo ba kung ano bibilhin nito?"
- Story cold-open: mid-scene, no context ("Anolin Arc")
- Callout: "Sa mga may negosyong gustong mag-expand…"
- Curiosity gap: "Ito ang unang tinitingnan ng investor bago pumirma."

Structure: Hook → tension/story → lesson → CTA. Soft CTA on teaching posts, hard CTA on offer posts ([[Education-First Content Engine]]).
