# Eugene Schwartz Awareness Stages

Match the message to the prospect's awareness:

| Stage | They know | Lead with |
|---|---|---|
| Unaware | Nothing | Story / emotion (the "Anolin Arc" posts) |
| Problem-aware | The pain | The pain, named ("Hindi ko na kaya branches") |
| Solution-aware | Solutions exist | Why systems, mechanism |
| Product-aware | Your offer | Proof, differentiation |
| Most aware | Ready | The deal, deadline |

FBPH content skews problem-aware → solution-aware; that's why problem-first ads outperform "franchise opportunity" ads.
