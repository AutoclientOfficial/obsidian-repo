# Funnel Fundamentals

Every funnel = traffic → capture → nurture → convert → ascend.

Our proven shape (validated in FBPH):

```text
Ad / Organic → Messenger flow → Free Assessment → Qualified? call : education product
```

Principles:

- **Segment with a quiz/assessment** — self-qualification beats form-filling
- **Two-path funnels monetize both outcomes** (DFY offer vs ebook)
- **Speed-to-lead**: automation answers in seconds, humans close
- Measure per stage: CPL, assessment completion, booking rate, show rate, close rate
