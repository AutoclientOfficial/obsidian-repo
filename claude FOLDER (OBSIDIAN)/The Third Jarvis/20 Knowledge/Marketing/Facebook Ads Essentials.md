# Facebook Ads Essentials

What matters for our PH B2B-ish audience:

- **Creative is the targeting.** Problem-first, owner-POV creative finds owners; interest stacks matter less than message-market fit.
- Campaign structure: 1 campaign / 2–3 ad sets / 3–5 creatives; kill losers weekly.
- Optimize for the deepest cheap event (messaging conversations or leads, not clicks).
- Metrics that matter: cost per qualified lead and cost per booked call — not CTR ([[Meta Ads Client Report Tool]] reads Meta exports).
- Avoid banned/hype phrasing ("guaranteed ROI," "passive income") — both for compliance and for lead quality ([[FBPH Marketing Strategy and Creative Direction]]).
