# Blue Ocean Strategy

Compete where nobody else is. Instead of fighting in red oceans (crowded markets), redefine the category.

Four actions: **Eliminate** (what the industry over-serves), **Reduce**, **Raise**, **Create** (what nobody offers).

FBPH application: everyone sells "franchise consulting" or "marketing services" — FBPH created "external expansion team with a guaranteed pipeline + 25% aligned upside." Different axis: alignment and outcomes, not deliverables.
