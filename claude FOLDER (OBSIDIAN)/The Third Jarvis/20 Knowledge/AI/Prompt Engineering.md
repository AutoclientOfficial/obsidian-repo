# Prompt Engineering

Core techniques that consistently improve output quality:

- **Be specific about role, audience, format, and length.** "Write a Taglish FB post for franchise-curious business owners, 100 words, ends with a CTA" beats "write a post."
- **Give examples (few-shot).** One good example of your voice outperforms paragraphs of description.
- **Ask for structure**: numbered steps, tables, or headers when output feeds a system.
- **Iterate in the same thread** — refine rather than restart.
- **Chain-of-thought**: for complex reasoning, ask the model to think step-by-step before answering.
- **Templates beat ad-hoc.** Store proven prompts in [[Prompt Library]].
