# AI Automation Stack

Common no-code/low-code layers for client automations:

| Tool | Best for | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| n8n | Complex workflows, self-hosted, cheap at scale | Steeper learning curve |
| Make | Visual scenarios, mid-complexity | Per-operation pricing |
| Zapier | Fastest setup, huge app catalog | Most expensive at volume |
| Botcake / ManyChat | FB Messenger flows (PH market standard) | Used in FBPH funnels |
| Google Apps Script | Sheets/Drive glue, webhooks | Free; deployment gotchas — see [[Quotation Form → Sheets → Payment Flow]] |

Principle: pick per client budget and volume; don't standardize on the most expensive tool.
