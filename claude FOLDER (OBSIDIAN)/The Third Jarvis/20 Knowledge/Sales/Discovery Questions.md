# Discovery Questions

Bank of questions for the FBPH-style discovery call:

- "Ano ang nag-trigger para mag-inquire ka ngayon?" (urgency)
- "May nagtanong na ba kung open for franchise kayo?" (demand proof)
- "Kung may partner kang handang mag-invest bukas, ano ang ipapakita mo?" (system gap — the money question)
- "Sino ang gumagawa ng sales/marketing ngayon?" (owner-dependency)
- "Magkano ang budget mo para sa expansion this year?" (BANT-B)
- "Ano ang mangyayari kung walang magbago in 12 months?" (implication)

Principle: diagnose before prescribing; the gap they admit becomes the offer we present.
