# Objection Handling

Framework: **Acknowledge → Isolate → Reframe → Confirm.**

Common FBPH objections and reframes:

| Objection | Reframe |
|---|---|
| "Mahal" | Compare vs hiring an in-house expansion team (₱150K+/mo payroll) — not vs doing nothing |
| "No prior agreement sa recurring fee" | Put every fee in the contract upfront ([[Money Principles]]); concede structure, not value |
| "Kaya ko naman mag-isa" | "Kaya mo — pero ilang buwan? Ano ang opportunity cost?" |
| "Walang guarantee?" | We guarantee the pipeline and the system; nobody honest guarantees ROI — that's your protection too |

Rule: never argue; isolate ("Kung ma-solve natin 'yan, go na?") before answering.
