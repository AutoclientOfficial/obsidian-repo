# High-Ticket Closing

- **Pre-frame beats persuasion.** Qualifier/disqualifier slides before price ([[Growth Partner Pitch Deck Structure]]).
- **Takeaway close**: "This is NOT for you if…" read slowly and confidently — prospects chase what might exclude them.
- **Price with silence.** State the number, stop talking.
- **Anchor with tiers**: showing Tier 2 makes Tier 1 feel accessible ([[FBPH Internal Marketing Agency Offers]]).
- **Down-payment closes commitment**: reservation fees (₱50K Eagles model) turn "interested" into "invested."
- Never chase; set the next step on the call or disqualify politely.
