# SPIN Selling

Four question types, in order, for consultative/high-ticket sales:

1. **Situation** — facts: "Ilang branches? Ano ang monthly sales?"
2. **Problem** — pains: "Ano ang humaharang sa expansion ngayon?"
3. **Implication** — cost of the pain: "Kung tuloy 'to for 12 months, magkano ang sayang na demand?"
4. **Need-payoff** — they state the value: "Kung may pipeline ka ng qualified partners, ano ang magbabago?"

Key insight: big-ticket buyers convince themselves through Implication and Need-payoff questions — don't pitch too early.
